### R7.3 `comet` (3I/ATLAS)

3I/ATLAS is a stretch target; the MVP demo body is 433 Eros (Q-137).

- **Requires:** `gas_Q` per epoch and `coma_radius_km`, filled from the published papers (Lisse et al.) with citations. Never guess them. In the R3.2 card shape `gas_Q` holds one epoch's values (Q-42), so it is used only when `epochs` lists exactly that epoch; with both epochs listed the shells are refused. The viewer opens on the latest epoch that has gas data.
- **Nucleus:** a dark sphere at `nucleus_radius_km_max`, labelled "upper size limit" (not for a registered mesh, see below): the scale bar, comparison and alt text all say "at most X across (upper limit)". If the value is missing or hidden by the honesty level, draw a point. The nucleus keeps one size across epochs when the coma radius is known.
- **Gas shells:** (issue #43: drawn as one emission **volume**, the coma, with one emitter per gas; the shells stay in the plan as data for the haptics) one gas emitter per gas, with density ∝ 1/r² out to the coma radius. Point count and brightness are proportional to Q for the selected epoch. Total points (three shells plus dust) stay at or under the 60k budget ([§5.5](../display-rules.md#r55-performance-budget-d-05)). Fixed false colours with a legend: CO₂ = cyan, H₂O = blue, CO = magenta. Tag `measured` (Q) + `model_dependent` (1/r² profile).
- **Dust:** a pear-shaped, Sun-pointing dust volume, **December only**.
- **Nucleus without a shape model (issue #71, Q-210):** a dark two-lobed outline (artistic layer `nucleus_shape`), lit like airless rock (`reflectance`, `model_dependent`). On the three.js path no jets are drawn; at + Artistic the D-37 rock engine draws the nucleus with the prototype's sunward jets (Q-301, Q-334; [R5.9](../engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37)), on the engine's two-lobed outline even for a mesh nucleus. A mesh nucleus (67P) is otherwise untouched.
- **Emissions view and modelled tail (issue #71 F, Q-260 to Q-263, amends the rule below):** the panel's "View emissions" opens the spectrum and gas bands on the left and the nucleus with gas particles on the right, with a time slider between the two epochs (`measured` inside an observing window, `model_dependent` between). A tail is drawn when the card has an `orbit_track`: the ion tail along the real anti-Sun direction (`derived`), the dust tail by Finson-Probstein with assumed grain sizes (`model_dependent`), both labelled as modelled. The earlier rule follows for a card without a track.
- **Review fixes (Q-333, 11 Nov 2026):**
  - **Water near perihelion:** one measured point from another instrument is added for 3I/ATLAS water: SOHO/SWAN, Q(H₂O) about 3.2 × 10²⁹ s⁻¹ on 6 Nov 2025 at 1.40 au (Combi et al., arXiv:2512.22354), labelled "measured by SOHO/SWAN, not SPHEREx", with no error carried (an independent analysis finds about 4 × 10²⁸, Tan, Yan and Li, arXiv:2601.15443). Between measured points the rate is a log-linear interpolation between the nearest measured values (`model_dependent`). A rate above the brightness scale's top is drawn at full brightness, and the note says so.
  - **Water break-up:** the Haser model, n ∝ exp(−r/L)/r² with L = v τ, v = 0.85 r_h^−0.5 km/s and τ = 8.3 × 10⁴ s × r_h² (both **[verify]**, R12 #50), sets the water shell's particle count and brightness (layer `h2o_breakup`, `model_dependent`); the drawn radial profile stays 1/r². CO₂ and CO keep the pure 1/r² shell.
  - **Dust tail:** each grain is integrated against a beta = 0 grain released at the same time, and only the difference is drawn, so the two-body drift from the Horizons path cancels. Grain sizes assume porous grains of 1 g/cm³ with Q_pr = 1 (compact grains are 2 to 3 times smaller for the same beta).
  - **Perihelion:** 2025-Oct-29.48 TDB at q = 1.356 au (JD 2460977.98).
- **Tail (without a track):** none in August. Draw a tail only if a published December result shows one: the layers `ion_tail` and `dust_tail` exist only when the backend offers them, and both need the Sun direction. The ion tail is straight along `-sun` (`derived`); the dust tail is curved (bend side is `artistic`: the card holds no orbital velocity). Lengths are not to scale. A comet whose card has a loadable `shape_source` (67P) draws its nucleus from that model as in R7.1 (Q-162): the coma, dust and tails are unchanged.
- **Nucleus mesh (67P, Q-162):** the registry row `c67p-shap5` (SBDB spkid `1000012`; SHAP5 050K as LOD0, the official 012K product as LOD1) types nothing differently: the object stays `comet` (R3.3.1) and only `facts.shape_model` carries the model. The render params get a `mesh` layer only when the card has `shape_source` **and** `nucleus_radius_km_max` (`measured` or the weaker of the two tags); without the size it is listed in `missing`, never guessed. A comet without a shape source has no `mesh` row at all (3I/ATLAS unchanged). The viewer scales the mesh so its bounding sphere (half the box diagonal, Q-43) has the card's nucleus radius; for 67P that sphere is 3.527 km (`bbox_half_diagonal_km` in the registry), against 1.648 km for the volume-equivalent radius, so a card radius taken from a mean diameter would draw the nucleus at 0.47 of its size. The card's value is therefore the mesh's own bounding radius: `build.py` sets `nucleus_radius_km_max` to the registry's `bbox_half_diagonal_km`, tagged `derived`, with a source that says it is the model's scaling radius and not a published radius (Q-43, Q-162), and `shape_source` from `facts.shape_model`. For such a comet the size is not called an upper limit (no `upper_limit` qualifier; the note says the nucleus is scaled to its model's bounding sphere); a comet without a mesh keeps the upper-limit wording. Frame: Cheops, +Z spin axis; the pole and 12.4041 h period are **pre-perihelion** values and change over perihelion, so the viewer does not spin the nucleus from them.
- **Epoch toggle:** Aug 2025 / Dec 2025. Clicking a shell highlights its spectral line, and clicking a line highlights its shell.
- **Haptics and sound:** field source ([§8.4](../haptics/sources.md#r84-event-sources)); its single tag is open (Q-17, R12 #20). The nucleus is a plain sphere with no height, so a comet has no body tone (R8.9.1); touching the nucleus gives one firm tick.
