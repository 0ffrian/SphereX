### R7.7 Shader-generated kinds: `planet_rocky`, `planet_water`, `gas_giant`, `nebula`, `nova`

**Spec**

- **Look:** browser shader generators with triplanar mapping, as in [architecture spec §6](../../architecture/generators.md) (D-01). This doc does not restate them.
- **Engine path (D-37, Q-287):** at + Artistic, rocky, water and gas worlds that `cardToWorld` accepts are drawn by the prototype's world engine on raw WebGL2 ([R5.9](../engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37)); everything below is the three.js path, which draws at + Estimated and Measured only and is the engine's fallback. Engine values are at the end of this section.
- **Shared CPU field (D-01, D-12):** planets and gas giants need a field the pointer can sample.
  - When a body loads, a Web Worker generates one equirectangular field at **512×256**. It runs once, not per frame. Moving storms are the exception: they are sampled from the shared model at the scene's t (R5.7, Q-105).
  - It uses the **same seed and the same functions** as the shader, so the field and the picture agree.
  - Six channels, packed into two RGBA `Float32` `DataTexture`s (D-12). The haptics read the same `Float32Array`s.
    - Texture A: R = height, G = band index, B = storm id, A = unused. For `gas_giant`, R holds the band profile instead (0.5 is a band edge): a gas giant has no relief (Q-48).
    - Texture B: R = ocean mask, G = ice mask, B = lava-crack mask, A = unused.
  - Pixel-to-direction mapping as in [§6.1](../surface-generation.md#r61-strategy-height-first-colour-second).
  - The field is uploaded to the shader as a `DataTexture`. The haptic sources ([§8.4](../haptics/sources.md#r84-event-sources)) and the body tone (R8.9.1) sample the **same array**. Principle 6 ("one height map drives everything") holds for these kinds too.
  - The shader may add finer visual noise below the field's resolution. That detail is seen but not heard or felt.
- **Nebulae and novae** (issue #43) are emission-only **volumes** in a box, with density from a deterministic 48³ noise cube baked on the CPU. The step counts and memory budget are owned by [§5.5](../display-rules.md#r55-performance-budget-d-05). They make no point sets and have no haptics or sound ([§8.5](../haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile) owns that rule).
  - The nova is the supernova remnant (Q-59): a smooth synchrotron core glow, thin filaments on a shell (H-alpha red, [O III] teal), dust absorption lanes and an outline from the size and axis ratio. No card field holds the axis ratio yet, so the outline is a circle.
  - The age slider moves the shell by the Sedov law `(t/t_max)^(2/5)` relative to the slider maximum (`artistic`). Phase names wait for transition times that are **[verify]** (R12 #24).
- **Gas giant layers (issue #62, Q-115):** a gas giant's surface is five layers, one per rung of the tier chain (D-32), each with its own switch: `bands` (Rhines count, A7.2; `inferred_from_analog`, tier 4: the card has no wind, so the typical 50 m/s is in `assumed`, and that value is unverified, so even a measured spin leaves the layer at tier 4, Q-119, Q-110; with no `rot_period_h` the typical spin is used and is in `assumed` too), `flattening` (backend: `f = f_J (P_J / P)^2`, Jupiter density assumed, `model_dependent` with a measured spin, `inferred_from_analog` with none; the viewer now draws the measured flattening for Jupiter and Saturn and `0.7 q` for other giants, so the two disagree, open as Q-332), `colour` (palette family from `teq_K`, `derived`; `artistic` default without it), `storms` (`artistic`: count and place are seeded) and `turbulence` (`artistic`). In Measured only none is drawn (a plain sphere of the measured radius), "+ Estimated" draws bands, flattening and colour, "+ Artistic" adds the storms and the turbulence, and the viewer's `jets` and `storm_drift` (artistic). Jupiter's period (9.925 h) and flattening (0.06487) are verified (NASA Jupiter Fact Sheet; IAU WGCCRE 2015; Q-119); the typical wind is a **[verify]** config value (R12 #42); all are config values (`gas_giant.py`, `shaderSpec.ts`; a backend test keeps the twins equal); `null` refuses the layer.
  - **What the legend says is what is drawn (PR #63 review, Q-115):** the viewer draws `bands` and `flattening` from the motion rates (`dynamics`, each with its own tag) and, above all, the sliders, which can be weaker than the backend layer (the backend knows only the card). So the legend's `bands` and `flattening` take the **weaker of the backend layer's tag and the viewer rule's tag** (`bandsRule`, `flatteningRule`), and their description, formula and `assumed` come from the rule that sets the drawn value (the band count quoted is the drawn one, pole to pole). A `bands` slider, or a flattening from the `mass_mearth` slider, is `artistic` (a plain-words line says it is the visitor's choice). The honesty switch for the layer uses the same weaker tag. The backend layer's refusals are kept: a layer the backend did not send (an older saved result) is refused with reason `backend` and listed in `missing`, never dropped. The plan reads the sliders from `PlanContext.overrides`.
- **Honesty:** the surfaces of planets, nebulae and novae are not measured, and every texture layer and texture haptic is `artistic` (a gas giant's own layers are the five above). The haptics filter is owned by [§8.2](../haptics/platform-encoding.md#r82-two-kinds-of-haptics-both-labelled-in-the-ui).
- **Haptics and sound:** terrain source (`planet_rocky`, `planet_water`) and flow source (`gas_giant`), [§8.4](../haptics/sources.md#r84-event-sources); profile rows in [§8.5](../haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile). On land the body tone follows the field's height (R8.9.1, Q-133). A gas giant has no height, so it keeps the flow cues (**Proposed (Q-191)**).
- **Motion (issue #42, Q-104):** gas giants have counter-flowing jets, storms that drift and turn, flattening from the spin and a band count from the Rhines rule. Worlds have clouds carried by latitude winds with cloud shadows, a single-scattering atmosphere rim, ocean Fresnel and Sun glint, and ice and ocean from the A7.1 Teq thresholds. Each sub-layer is listed with its tier; nothing moves without a stated speed-up (R5.7).
- **Lava:** `planet_rocky` lava cracks glow only when `teq_K` is known and above about 700 K ([A7.1](../../architecture/physics.md)); the ramp to full strength at 1500 K is **[verify]**, artistic (R12 #25).
- **Status:** D-12 is **Accepted** (Atish, 5 Nov 2026). The jet pulse rate is **[verify by ear on the A15]**, and by touch after the event (R12 #14).
- **Visual findings (issue #42, Q-107):** the hard, straight terminator on gas giant, rocky and water worlds is fixed (PR #63 review, see [R5.1](../display-rules.md#r51-lighting): the bare `sun_dir` hint is turned toward the camera, the gas Minnaert term is floored and a tiny night floor keeps the dark half readable); the water world's missing blue ocean was fixed earlier. The storm-channel amendment of D-12 is Q-105.

**As built (issues #8, #42, #43)**

- **Field:** `viewer/fields/fieldGenerator.ts` (pure, seeded, integer-hash noise) is the source of truth; `fieldWorker.ts` wraps it and `fieldClient.ts` falls back to the main thread. `FieldSampler` is the typed sampler for haptic sources. The worker's `points` kind stays for a later CPU twin of the noise cube; nothing requests it now.
- **Shader:** reads the two textures with `textureLoad` and filters by hand, so float32 filtering support does not matter. It recomputes only sub-resolution noise and the analytic storm shapes (centres from `stormsFor`, the same function the field uses). Terrain R is the raw terrain height; consumers flatten the ocean with the ocean mask.
- **Shared with the shader, not packed:** `zonalWind(d, params)` (counter-flowing jets, the same warped band phase as the drawn bands), the ring geometry and `ringGapAt`, and the storm ring constants behind `STORM_EDGE_FACTOR` (1.9, was 1.5; the felt storm area grows about 1.6×, signed off by Atish, D-13), so the felt edge is the drawn edge (Q-48). Moving storms come from `fields/flowModel.ts`, handed to the shader and to `FieldSampler.setStorms` at the same t (Q-105).
- **Starlight and climate scenario (issue #68, Q-155 to Q-157):**
  - Layer `starlight` (`PlanBuilder.draw`, viewer-added; requires `host_teff_K`): the DirectionalLight colour is `planLighting(...).colour`; `ShaderBody.setSunColour` multiplies it into the atmosphere rim and sky glint. Cleared to white when the layer is hidden or refused.
  - Layer `climate` now names the scenario: `shaderSpec.ts::deriveClimate` (via `physics/climate.ts`) runs for a card that carries the host-star fields and a `teq_K`. `eyeball` and `snowball` replace the polar-ice rule and set `FieldParams.substellar` and `iceAngleRad`; `temperate` keeps the rule, weakened to the locking tag; `lava` and `hot_dry` keep the rule as it was. A refused locking rule lists `tidal_locking` in `missing` with the field it lacked. Both demo planets are locked (Q-155, ruling of 10 Nov 2026): TRAPPIST-1 f and Kepler-186 f (3.986 against 4.0 Gyr, a knife edge) show the eyeball pattern at + Estimated; a `temperate` plan with no pattern needs an unlocked planet.
  - Field (`fieldGenerator.ts`): in substellar mode ice is `smoothstep` over the angle from +X (longitude 0, the middle column) with the seeded `wob` edge, ocean stays where the terrain is below sea level inside the cap, ice covers everything outside it (the night side included), `iceAngleRad` 0 is a snowball (a water world is all ice, bare rock keeps a little high ground bare), and `iceExtent` is ignored. Without `substellar` the bytes are those of the polar rule. The params travel whole through the worker, the client fallback and the main thread, so the three paths give the same bytes.
  - Climate pattern at + Estimated: when the artistic surface is hidden and the scenario is `eyeball` or `snowball`, `plan.shader.pattern` is true (the climate layer is drawn from its own tag) and `ShaderBody` switches its pattern mode on from the visible tags: bump, colour, clouds, atmosphere and lava are replaced by the field's ocean and ice masks in `PATTERN_GROUND`, `PATTERN_WATER`, `PATTERN_ICE` (fixed key colours, not a claim about the surface), on a smooth sphere at the measured radius. The pattern is drawn from the ice mask alone: a uniform open-cap colour (water or ground) inside the cap and ice outside it, so the ocean mask's noise-derived coastline and the snowball's bare high rock (both artistic terrain) are ignored; a snowball is uniform ice. The locked pose and the no-spin rule apply. At Measured only the plain grey sphere stays, in white light. Nothing `artistic` is visible at + Estimated.
  - Scenario looks and presets (issue #68 Part 4 and 5, Q-159, Q-160): when the scenario is known, `shaderSpec.ts` replaces `PLANET_DEFAULTS[kind]` with `mergeLook(lookForScenario(scenario), preset?.look)`, then the sliders (sliders win, D-27). It sets `hue` (`surfaceHue`, 0..1 on the `PlanetStyle.hue` scale), `relief`, `cloudCover`, `atmosphere` (`atmosphereStrength`), `atmosphereColour` (linear RGB) and the field `freq`. The preset is looked up by the archive `pl_name` (`card.name`) in `looks/presets.ts` (`PRESETS` is empty until tuned and approved). Layers `look` and `look_preset` (legends `lookLegend` and `presetLegend`) are `artistic`, so they show at + Artistic only, with the generated surface. With no scenario, and for gas giants, the defaults are drawn as before. `cloudStyle` and `iceContrast` are part of a look but not drawn: the shader has no control for them (open).
  - **Mould steps, notes and alt text (issue #68 Part 6, Q-166):** the `rocky` and `water` steps reveal `starlight` in the first step after Base mould (Terrain) and `climate`, `look`, `look_preset` in Colour; Base mould names `body` as well as `surface`. `planCommon.ts::exoplanetSentences` writes the sentences for the plan's notes and for `altText.ts`: the starlight is an estimate from the star's temperature, the climate is a model scenario, never a measured surface, and a hidden layer says which level shows it ("choose + Estimated to see ...", Q-115 wording). The two demo planets (`planet_trappist1f`, `planet_kepler186f`, real archive values, Q-165) show: Measured only a plain grey sphere in white light; + Estimated the star-coloured light and, both being locked at the Q-155 constants, the eyeball pattern on a smooth sphere; + Artistic the surface with the scenario look.
- **Determinism is per engine:** the noise uses only exactly specified operations, but the field also calls `Math.sin`, `cos`, `acos`, `atan2` and `pow`, which ECMAScript only approximates, so two engines can differ by an ulp and flip a lattice cell. The tests run on V8 only.
- **Seeds:** `RenderParams.seed` is reduced with `boundedSeed` (mod 997, in `shaderSeedOffsets`) before it is added to float32 shader noise; the integer-hash field uses the low 32 bits (`hash3` takes `seed | 0`). The backend seed is 32-bit today (R6.3), so nothing is lost (Q-35).
- **Lighting:** gas giants are lit by the scene's `DirectionalLight` like the planets. The direction comes from `planLighting(params, card).direction` (phase angle, else `sun_dir`, else head-on `[0,0,1]`; none when self-lit), the same value for the scene sun and the body.
- **Entry point:** the same path as the baked kinds, `ViewerApp.setObject` → `planRender` → `buildScene`. `renderers/shaderPlan.ts` plans them with `PlanBuilder` (legend, `missing`, notes, scale bar). `buildScene` creates the `ShaderBody` from `plan.shader` (`ShaderBody.start`: the plain sphere is there at once and the surface swaps in when the field is ready, so the view is never blank), and `ViewerApp.body` exposes it to haptics. The body gets `visibleTags` from `allowedTags(plan.honesty)`, its light from the plan, and `onChange` from `BuildHooks`. A plan whose `sceneKey` is unchanged calls `BuiltScene.update(plan)`: the field is **not** regenerated on an honesty toggle. A failed field is reported through `onLoadFailed('surface')`, so the plan refuses the layer with reason `load`. `ShaderBody.setTime(time)` poses the body from the SimClock.
- **Plain sphere (Q-45):** when the surface is hidden (for a gas giant: at Measured only, Q-115), planets and gas giants keep a plain grey sphere only while the size on the card (`radius_rjup`) is shown at the honesty level. Nebula and nova have no plain sphere and draw nothing while hidden. The plan (`plan.shader.plain`) and the body apply the same rule.
- **`BodyRenderer` (plug-in shape):** `field` (a `FieldSampler`, null for the nebula and the nova), `pointSets` (null for every kind now), `worldToBody(p)` (undoes the gas-giant tilt and group scale), `status` (drawn / hidden / refused with a plain-words message and tags), `altText`, and `ctx.onChange` so a render-on-change viewer redraws. A rebuild builds the new surface before dropping the old one (never blank). The tag chip and badge come from the plan's legend (`plan.drawn`), not from `status`. A standalone body defaults `visibleTags` to + Estimated (Q-49).
- **Refusal:** layer `surface` (planets), the five gas giant layers, or `point_cloud` (nebula, nova; the id is kept, the layer is drawn as a volume) is refused and listed in `missing` when `scale_m` or the size field is null, or when the backend lists it in `RenderParams.missing`. A gas giant layer the backend did not send (an older saved result) is refused with reason `backend` (Q-115).
- **Volumes (method):** ray-marched in a box with per-pixel `interleavedGradientNoise` jitter (TAAU smooths it). The noise cube (`volume/noiseVolume.ts`) is baked in time slices of about 6 ms (`bakeNoiseVolumeSliced`, the same bytes as the one-shot `bakeNoiseVolume`, cached by seed), so no long task blocks the page. The volume is empty until the cube arrives; then the viewer redraws from a fresh temporal history (`onVolumeNoiseReady`) and `whenSettled` waits for the bake. The ray start shifts per frame by the TAAU frame index (`jitterShiftForFrame`, golden-ratio steps; index 0 after a restart, so a settled frame is deterministic). Volumes are depth-tested against opaque bodies but never write depth; the test uses the far face of the box, so a pixel the nucleus covers also loses the thin volume in front of it.

**Gas giants that run as a simulation (issue #71, Q-250 to Q-259).** `viewer/renderers/GasWorld.ts` replaces the painted band shader when the card has the data (`gasWorldInput`); otherwise `ShaderBody` draws as before. The planet is hidden with the message "Letting the winds settle..." for 420 solver steps, so it appears already formed. Bands stay where they are observed (Jupiter, Saturn) or come from the Rhines count; colour tracers are carried by the solved flow in a half-float render target (`BodyRenderer.prepareFrame`). Pacing is the clock's `winds` process: 5 minutes of planet time per second by default, one day per second with the panel's time-speed buttons (`gas_pacing`); in one-day mode, and for a locked giant, the view does not spin. Saturn's rings use the NASA ring radii, with shadows both ways. The felt wind, storms and ring edges come from `FieldSampler.flow`, the same solution that is drawn. `GasWorld.orbitSources` hands the live textures to an orbit camera. Since issue #93 (Q-348) its tracer noise is one copy over the sphere, filled from sphere directions with the seed (it was a texture tiled 4 × 3), so every giant drawn on this path looks different from before.
