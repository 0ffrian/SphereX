# Renderers: shader-generated kinds

> **Part of:** Rendering & haptics spec ([index](../README.md)) · **Sections:** R7.7 · **Implements:** [D-01, Q-45, Q-48, Q-49, Q-59](../../decisions/rendering-bodies.md), [D-04, D-21, Q-35](../../decisions/contract.md), [D-12, Q-133](../../decisions/haptics-design.md), [Q-103, Q-104, Q-107](../../decisions/rendering-motion.md), [Q-105](../../decisions/haptics-built.md) · **Read with:** [generators.md](../../architecture/generators.md) (A6, the look), [haptics/sources.md](../haptics/sources.md) (R8.4), [display-rules.md](../display-rules.md) (R5.5 volume budget)

### R7.7 Shader-generated kinds: `planet_rocky`, `planet_water`, `gas_giant`, `nebula`, `nova`

**Spec**

- **Look:** browser shader generators with triplanar mapping, as in [architecture spec §6](../../architecture/generators.md) (D-01). This doc does not restate them.
- **Shared CPU field (D-01, D-12):** planets and gas giants need a field the pointer can sample.
  - When a body loads, a Web Worker generates one equirectangular field at **512×256**. It runs once, not per frame. Moving storms are the exception: they are sampled from the shared model at the scene's t (R5.7, Q-105).
  - It uses the **same seed and the same functions** as the shader, so the field and the picture agree.
  - Six channels, packed into two RGBA `Float32` `DataTexture`s (D-12). The haptics read the same `Float32Array`s.
    - Texture A: R = height, G = band index, B = storm id, A = unused. For `gas_giant`, R holds the band profile instead (0.5 is a band edge): a gas giant has no relief (Q-48).
    - Texture B: R = ocean mask, G = ice mask, B = lava-crack mask, A = unused.
  - Pixel-to-direction mapping as in [§6.1](../surface-generation.md#r61-strategy-height-first-colour-second).
  - The field is uploaded to the shader as a `DataTexture`. The haptic sources ([§8.4](../haptics/sources.md#r84-event-sources)) and the body tone (R8.9.1) sample the **same array**. Principle 6 ("one height map drives everything") holds for these kinds too.
  - The shader may add finer visual noise below the field's resolution. That detail is seen but not heard or felt.
- **Nebulae and novae** (issue #43) are emission-only **volumes** in a box, with density from a deterministic 48³ noise cube baked on the CPU. The step counts and memory budget are owned by [§5.5](../display-rules.md#r55-performance-budget-d-05). They make no point sets and have no haptics or sound ([§8.5](../haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile) owns that rule).
  - The nova is the supernova remnant (Q-59): a smooth synchrotron core glow, thin filaments on a shell (H-alpha red, [O III] teal), dust absorption lanes and an outline from the size and axis ratio. No card field holds the axis ratio yet, so the outline is a circle.
  - The age slider moves the shell by the Sedov law `(t/t_max)^(2/5)` relative to the slider maximum (`artistic`). Phase names wait for transition times that are **[verify]** (R12 #24).
- **Honesty:** nothing about these surfaces is measured. Every texture layer and texture haptic is `artistic`. The haptics filter is owned by [§8.2](../haptics/platform-encoding.md#r82-two-kinds-of-haptics-both-labelled-in-the-ui).
- **Haptics and sound:** terrain source (`planet_rocky`, `planet_water`) and flow source (`gas_giant`), [§8.4](../haptics/sources.md#r84-event-sources); profile rows in [§8.5](../haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile). On land the body tone follows the field's height (R8.9.1, Q-133). A gas giant has no height, so it keeps the flow cues (**Proposed (Q-NEW-REN-4)**).
- **Motion (issue #42, Q-104):** gas giants have counter-flowing jets, storms that drift and turn, flattening from the spin and a band count from the Rhines rule. Worlds have clouds carried by latitude winds with cloud shadows, a single-scattering atmosphere rim, ocean Fresnel and Sun glint, and ice and ocean from the A7.1 Teq thresholds. Each sub-layer is listed with its tier; nothing moves without a stated speed-up (R5.7).
- **Lava:** `planet_rocky` lava cracks glow only when `teq_K` is known and above about 700 K ([A7.1](../../architecture/physics.md)); the ramp to full strength at 1500 K is **[verify]**, artistic (R12 #25).
- **Status:** D-12 is **Accepted** (Atish, 5 Nov 2026). The jet pulse rate is **[verify by ear on the A15]**, and by touch after the event (R12 #14).
- **Open visual findings (issue #42, Q-107):** a hard, straight terminator on gas giant, rocky and water worlds, and a water world without the blue ocean of its reference. The storm-channel amendment of D-12 is Q-105.

**As built (issues #8, #42, #43)**

- **Field:** `viewer/fields/fieldGenerator.ts` (pure, seeded, integer-hash noise) is the source of truth; `fieldWorker.ts` wraps it and `fieldClient.ts` falls back to the main thread. `FieldSampler` is the typed sampler for haptic sources. The worker's `points` kind stays for a later CPU twin of the noise cube; nothing requests it now.
- **Shader:** reads the two textures with `textureLoad` and filters by hand, so float32 filtering support does not matter. It recomputes only sub-resolution noise and the analytic storm shapes (centres from `stormsFor`, the same function the field uses). Terrain R is the raw terrain height; consumers flatten the ocean with the ocean mask.
- **Shared with the shader, not packed:** `zonalWind(d, params)` (counter-flowing jets, the same warped band phase as the drawn bands), the ring geometry and `ringGapAt`, and the storm ring constants behind `STORM_EDGE_FACTOR` (1.9, was 1.5; the felt storm area grows about 1.6×, signed off by Atish, D-13), so the felt edge is the drawn edge (Q-48). Moving storms come from `fields/flowModel.ts`, handed to the shader and to `FieldSampler.setStorms` at the same t (Q-105).
- **Determinism is per engine:** the noise uses only exactly specified operations, but the field also calls `Math.sin`, `cos`, `acos`, `atan2` and `pow`, which ECMAScript only approximates, so two engines can differ by an ulp and flip a lattice cell. The tests run on V8 only.
- **Seeds:** `RenderParams.seed` is reduced with `boundedSeed` (mod 997, in `shaderSeedOffsets`) before it is added to float32 shader noise; the integer-hash field uses the low 32 bits (`hash3` takes `seed | 0`). The backend seed is 32-bit today (R6.3), so nothing is lost (Q-35).
- **Lighting:** gas giants are lit by the scene's `DirectionalLight` like the planets. The direction comes from `planLighting(params, card).direction` (phase angle, else `sun_dir`, else head-on `[0,0,1]`; none when self-lit), the same value for the scene sun and the body.
- **Entry point:** the same path as the baked kinds, `ViewerApp.setObject` → `planRender` → `buildScene`. `renderers/shaderPlan.ts` plans them with `PlanBuilder` (legend, `missing`, notes, scale bar). `buildScene` creates the `ShaderBody` from `plan.shader` (`ShaderBody.start`: the plain sphere is there at once and the surface swaps in when the field is ready, so the view is never blank), and `ViewerApp.body` exposes it to haptics. The body gets `visibleTags` from `allowedTags(plan.honesty)`, its light from the plan, and `onChange` from `BuildHooks`. A plan whose `sceneKey` is unchanged calls `BuiltScene.update(plan)`: the field is **not** regenerated on an honesty toggle. A failed field is reported through `onLoadFailed('surface')`, so the plan refuses the layer with reason `load`. `ShaderBody.setTime(time)` poses the body from the SimClock.
- **Plain sphere (Q-45):** when the surface is hidden, planets and gas giants keep a plain grey sphere only while the size on the card (`radius_rjup`) is shown at the honesty level. Nebula and nova have no plain sphere and draw nothing while hidden. The plan (`plan.shader.plain`) and the body apply the same rule.
- **`BodyRenderer` (plug-in shape):** `field` (a `FieldSampler`, null for the nebula and the nova), `pointSets` (null for every kind now), `worldToBody(p)` (undoes the gas-giant tilt and group scale), `status` (drawn / hidden / refused with a plain-words message and tags), `altText`, and `ctx.onChange` so a render-on-change viewer redraws. A rebuild builds the new surface before dropping the old one (never blank). The tag chip and badge come from the plan's legend (`plan.drawn`), not from `status`. A standalone body defaults `visibleTags` to + Estimated (Q-49).
- **Refusal:** layer `surface` (planets, gas giants) or `point_cloud` (nebula, nova; the id is kept, the layer is drawn as a volume) is refused and listed in `missing` when `scale_m` or the size field is null, or when the backend lists it in `RenderParams.missing`.
- **Volumes (method):** ray-marched in a box with per-pixel `interleavedGradientNoise` jitter (TAAU smooths it). The noise cube (`volume/noiseVolume.ts`) is baked in time slices of about 6 ms (`bakeNoiseVolumeSliced`, the same bytes as the one-shot `bakeNoiseVolume`, cached by seed), so no long task blocks the page. The volume is empty until the cube arrives; then the viewer redraws from a fresh temporal history (`onVolumeNoiseReady`) and `whenSettled` waits for the bake. The ray start shifts per frame by the TAAU frame index (`jitterShiftForFrame`, golden-ratio steps; index 0 after a restart, so a settled frame is deterministic). Volumes are depth-tested against opaque bodies but never write depth; the test uses the far face of the box, so a pixel the nucleus covers also loses the thin volume in front of it.
