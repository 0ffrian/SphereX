### R7.7.1 World engine values

Split out of [R7.7](shader-kinds.md#r77-shader-generated-kinds-planet_rocky-planet_water-gas_giant-nebula-nova) on 11 Nov 2026 (that fragment had reached 21 KB); the first block below is moved verbatim, then amended for issue #93.

**World engine values (D-37; Q-288, Q-304, Q-313 to Q-316, Q-330, Q-331).** The prototype's own code (`frontend/src/engine/worlds/`), fed by `cardToWorld`; reviewed fixes are `@deviation` blocks (Q-335).

- **Card against preset:** for an object the prototype has (TRAPPIST-1 e and f, Kepler-186 f, K2-18 b, HD 189733 b, Jupiter, Saturn, Titan) the preset is drawn whole; a preset value keeps the card's tag only within 0.5 % of the card, otherwise it is `inferred_from_analog` with "the card says X". Other cards use the prototype's defaults (water, 1 bar, mu 29, sea share 0.5), each `model_dependent` and listed; a `planet_water` card is an ocean world (Q-288). Titan uses one heliocentric orbit (P is Saturn's year, `derived`); its 15.95-day orbit around Saturn is its spin.
- **Albedo (Q-359 amends Q-330):** inverted from the card's measured surface temperature so the engine's mean surface temperature matches it (`derived`, Titan); a catalogue `teq_K` is not inverted (it assumes an albedo itself); else the card's albedo (`model_dependent`; a geometric albedo is not a Bond albedo); else 0.3, listed as an assumption.
- **Lava worlds:** a rocky world with no pressure on its card and Teq of at least 1,400 K (basalt solidus) gets a thin rock-vapour atmosphere (0.005 bar, mu 44, no sea), `inferred_from_analog`; the citations for millibar rock-vapour atmospheres are **[verify]** (R12 #48). 55 Cnc e is drawn airless with its magma showing.
- **Clouds:** water condenses at a mixing ratio of 0.01 (Earth's cloud base about 0.72 km); the fly view's cloud deck sits at the model's cloud base, at least 0.3 km.
- **CO₂:** its 1 atm point is a sublimation point, so the liquid–vapour line starts at the triple point (Clausius–Clapeyron with Lv about 15.3 kJ/mol, **[verify]**, R12 #46) and ends at the critical point (304.13 K, 7.3773 MPa). Above the triple point its saturation pressure uses the same `Lv` (Q-356).
- **Gas giants:** Jupiter and Saturn use their measured peak jets (about 150 and 450 m/s, **[verify]**, R12 #47) and measured flattening (0.06487, 0.09796); other giants use U = 0.6 √(R dT) with a 10 K floor (about 115 to 120 m/s, an estimate) and f = 0.7 q, both `model_dependent`. Ring and planet shadows use the flattened shape. Great Red Spot about 11,000 × 16,350 km (Simon et al. 2024); cyclones spin cyclonically; band latitudes are planetographic.
- **Saturn's rings:** Encke Gap centred at 133,584 km (2.2165 R_S), about 325 km wide; F ring at 140,180 to 140,240 km (one texture column), from the PDS Ring-Moon Systems Node table, **[verify]** (R12 #47). The TSL fallback uses the same radii (`ENCKE_GAP_KM`); `classifyRing` names the Encke Gap and the Cassini Division for the haptics.
- **Solver pacing (TSL `GasWorld`):** one step is about 1,525 s of planet time for Jupiter and 430 s for Saturn (Q-331 corrects Q-254).

**Issue #93 physics (D-38; Q-355 to Q-361).** Each is a `@deviation (#93 3.x)` in the engine (`world_engine_deviations.py`) with the same rule in `frontend/src/physics/`.

- **Critical points (Q-355):** water 647.10 K, 22.064 MPa; NH₃ 405.4 K, 11.33 MPa; N₂ 126.19 K, 3.3958 MPa; CH₄ 190.56 K, 4.599 MPa; CO₂ 304.13 K, 7.3773 MPa (pure-fluid handbook values, NIST Chemistry WebBook). The boiling point is capped at Tc; above both Tc and Pc the fluid is `supercritical` (no sea surface) and the caption says so. NH₃–H₂O uses pure NH₃'s critical point, **[verify]** (R12 #53).
- **Saturation pressure (Q-356):** water by Murphy and Koop 2005 (eq. 7 over ice, eq. 10 over liquid; 1,706 Pa at 288 K); CO₂ above its triple point with `Lv` (1.61 MPa at 250 K, NIST 1.785 MPa), below it with the sublimation heat.
- **Tides and orbit (Q-357, Q-363):** the tidal heating, eccentricity and obliquity read the preset's own `p.ob` (host mass from `host_mass_msun`, eccentricity from `orbit_ecc`) before the prototype's `ORB` table. The starlight uses the host luminosity (`host_lum_lsun`, the star radius set to √L (5772 / T)²) when the card has it, else `insolation_searth`, else `host_radius_rsun` (R3.2).
- **Runaway greenhouse (Q-360):** the inner edge is Kopparapu et al. 2014 for the planet's mass (0.1, 1 or 5 Earth masses, interpolated in log mass); during a runaway the outgoing radiation is held at 282 W m⁻² (Goldblatt et al. 2013) until the surface reaches the post-runaway temperature, 1,500 K at 50 bar to 1,800 K at 260 bar of steam interpolated in ln P, with the steam pressure taken as the surface pressure, **[verify]** (R12 #51). K2-18 b: 401 K before, about 1,167 K now, drawn as lava under steam haze.
- **Climate budget (Q-361, open):** the heat-transport and sea-ice fix of #93 3.7 is not merged; the engine keeps the prototype's heat transport and sea ice, and `physics/climateBudget.ts` has drifted from it (freezing point, cloud constants, snow-melt rate).
- **Phase-curve readouts (Q-363):** `dayside_temp_K`, `nightside_temp_K` and `hotspot_offset_deg` from the card are shown as readouts beside the engine's own model numbers; they are not engine inputs.
- **Gas solver (Q-343):** at most 3 steps a frame; a giant that needs more runs slower than one day per second and the caption states the real rate at 60 fps (HD 189733 b about 15 h per second).
- **Seeds and patterns (Q-347 to Q-352):** see [R5.9](../engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37).
