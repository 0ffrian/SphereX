## R9. Accessibility tie-ins

> **Changed by [D-38](../decisions/shell-surfaces.md#d-38-design-language-graphite-inter-tight-radii-animate-ui), [Q-411](../decisions/shell-surfaces.md) and [Q-413](../decisions/shell-surfaces.md) (PR #104):** the Pause button is removed from the Details pane. The `P` key in the build pane pauses and resumes motion (and starts it under reduced motion), announced in the live region and ignored while typing; Details has a motion toggle. Reduced motion still stops auto-spin. The island's idle loader pulses softly (opacity 0.4 to 1, 3 s) and stays still under reduced motion (Q-413). Both are open against WCAG 2.2.2 (Q-411, Q-413).

- **Alt text** (from the card): "<name>, <type>, <size + tag>, <colour basis>, viewed at <phase angle>°. Surface relief: <measured | illustrative>." Update `aria-label` on the canvas whenever the mode changes.
- `aria-live="polite"` announces mode, epoch and honesty-state changes, the build's start and end ("Building <name>", "Built <name>") and the build's honesty raise ("Showing artist's impression so the whole body can be built", R5.8).
- All controls are ≥24×24 CSS px and keyboard reachable. The 3D view has on-screen rotate buttons as an alternative to dragging (WCAG 2.5.7, R5.6). Feel mode works from the keyboard too (R5.6).
- **Reduced motion.** `prefers-reduced-motion` disables auto-spin and pauses every simulation clock: the scene starts paused, with the pause button still available ([§5.7](display-rules.md#r57-motion-and-time-lapse), D-34). The build pane shows the built body at once, with no moulding and no auto-turn (R5.8). Sound is not motion: the body tone and the Listen sweep still play.
- **Focus.** Opening the build pane moves focus to its title; closing it moves focus to the selected object's sky marker, or to the sky canvas when there is none (`app/build/focus.ts`).
- **Nothing only by touch or sound.** Haptics and sounds never carry information that is not also available as text or as a picture. At the event there is no vibration (Q-132), so every haptic event is heard through the audio driver ([§8.9](haptics/audio-pairing.md#r89-audio-pairing-d-10)). The body tone (R8.9.1) repeats the relief that is drawn; the Track path sound (R8.9.2) repeats the drawn path, and the direction of travel is also given in text, never by stereo pan alone.
- A body with no touch or sound feedback says so in plain words in the 3D view's notes (for example "Nebulae have no touch feedback", R8.5), so Feel on it is not silent without a reason.
- **Search combobox (issue #68, Q-161).** The search field is an ARIA combobox: the input has `role="combobox"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` and `aria-activedescendant`; the list is `role="listbox"` and each row `role="option"` with `aria-selected`. Focus never leaves the input. The list opens on focus, click, tap or "/", and when the user types. Arrow Up and Arrow Down move the highlight, Enter picks the highlighted row (with no highlight, Enter searches the typed text), Escape closes the list and keeps the text. It closes on a pick, on submit, and when focus or a click leaves the field. Filtering ignores case, apostrophes and spaces and checks the name and every alias. Hints: "No demo object matches. Press Enter to search the catalogues." and `Try "WISE 0855" or "269.452, 4.693"`. A row shows its name and, only when the pre-built card gives one, a class label; never a physical value.
- **Search list on a phone (width ≤ 640 px).** The list opens above the pill, out of the layout flow, so opening it never moves the pill. It has its own `max-height` and scroll, and each row is at least 44 px tall. The one-line help above the pill is hidden while the list is open (it stays available to screen readers through `aria-describedby`).
