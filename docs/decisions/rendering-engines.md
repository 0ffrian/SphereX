## D-37 The approved prototype engine is the renderer for generated bodies (Decided by Moon, 11 Nov 2026)

Moon: "we already created their 3d model in the artifact, can't we just port them to the repo ... the processing needs to work exactly like in the artifact ... but the render needs to also work in case someone inputs their own exoplanet." Decision: the approved prototype engine (`sculptor-preview-next.html`: NOISE, ADV_FS, GAS_FS, WORLD_FS, FLY_FS, FLUIDS, worldModel, terrain build, routeTerrain, hydroRaster, EB, GasSim, gasProfile, GAS_NODES, WorldGL, the fly camera helpers and the tick and spin-up logic) moves verbatim into `frontend/src/engine/worlds/` (only TypeScript wrapping, imports and exports, and the removal of DOM and UI code) and renders rocky, water and gas worlds. It is raw WebGL2 on its own canvas, which knowingly overrides the "three r187 TSL only" rule for these bodies; the rest of the app stays three r187. A `cardToWorld(card, overrides)` adapter builds the prototype's preset object from any card (same assumptions as the prototype, tagged `model_dependent` and listed; a missing required input refuses with the D-32 fallback). This supersedes the TSL port of Q-286 (the TSL `RockyWorld.ts` of commit 49bfc63 stays as the fallback until the engine is hosted). Q-287 (visible at "+ Estimated") and Q-288 (ocean basin) still apply to the adapter. Status: Decided (Moon); being built. Stream W of #71 (branch `feat/71-w-engine`) extracts the engine into `frontend/src/engine/worlds/` with `frontend/scripts/extract-world-engine.py` and adds `cardToWorld` and the parity page (see Q-303 to Q-306); hosting in the app UI is the next stream.

- **Amends:** D-03 (acknowledged by Atish, 11 Nov 2026). The D-37 engines (the prototype's JavaScript and GLSL on raw WebGL2: world, star and rock) run alongside three.js r187 TSL, which stays the viewer and the fallback path. CLAUDE.md "Rules for code" says the same.
- **Amended by:** Q-287 (Atish, 11 Nov 2026): the engines mount only at "+ Artistic"; "visible at + Estimated" above no longer holds.
- **Scope as built (11 Nov 2026):** three engines, each on its own WebGL2 canvas: `WorldEngine` (`frontend/src/engine/worlds/`, rocky, water and gas worlds; hosted by `WorldGlobeStage` and `WorldFlyStage` in `app/WorldEngineStage.tsx`, Q-313 to Q-316), `StarEngine` (`engine/stars/`, hosted by `app/StarEngineStage.tsx`, Q-309 to Q-312) and `RockView` (`engine/rocks/`, hosted by `app/build/RockStage.tsx` and `RockCloseUp`, Q-299 to Q-302). While one draws the body, the three.js viewer builds and draws nothing under it (`ViewerApp.setEngineOwned`).
- **The three.js renderers stay (Atish, 11 Nov 2026, PR #89 review):** `GasWorld.ts`, `RockyWorld.ts`, `viewer/orbit/*` and the other TSL renderers are the fallback. They draw at "+ Estimated" and "Measured only" (Q-287), and whenever the adapter refuses the card, an engine fails to start, loses its WebGL context, or WebGL2 is missing. They are not dropped. Spec: [R5.9](../rendering/engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37).
- **Determinism exemption (Atish, 11 Nov 2026):** engine frames are not byte-identical: the spin-up runs on the wall clock (Q-306), the render scale adapts to frame time, and the star engine uses the prototype's fixed seeds, not the card seed. The byte-identical rule (R1 principle 5) applies to the baked textures, which still pass `bake_demo_pack --check`, `bake_fixtures --check` and `bake_reference_objects --check`, and to the card and RenderParams. This narrows D-36's "simulations are deterministic" for engine frames. Spec: [R5.9](../rendering/engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37).
- **Amended by:** D-38 (issue #93, Atish, 11 Nov 2026), in [rendering-engines-tuning.md](rendering-engines-tuning.md): a card star's surface and spots now take a seed from the card id (Q-349); the world shaders compile in the background with measured compile times (Q-337, Q-338); the world engine's pixel ratio and scaler changed (Q-342). The A15 frame-rate check below is still open.
- **Frame rate not measured:** the A15 check of at least 30 fps while rotating (D-05, [R5.5](../rendering/display-rules.md#r55-performance-budget-d-05), [R11](../rendering/acceptance-tests.md#r11-acceptance-tests)) has not been run for the rock engine or for the two world-engine instances (globe and fly view, Q-313). It must be done on the A15 before the event (R12 #43, A14.1).
- **Known limitation:** the star engine runs its slices through `new Function`, so a Content Security Policy needs `'unsafe-eval'` in `script-src` (Q-329).
- **Review fixes (PR #89, 11 Nov 2026):** Q-329 (star), Q-330 (worlds), Q-331 (gas giants), Q-334 (rocks), Q-335 (verbatim convention and extractors), in [rendering-engines-review.md](rendering-engines-review.md).
- **Status:** Decided (Moon); amends D-03 (acknowledged by Atish, 11 Nov 2026); built.

## Q-286 to Q-288 (the world adapter: TSL port, honesty level, ocean basin)

D-37 cited these three IDs, but no entry was written for them. They are recorded here on 11 Nov 2026, reconstructed from D-37, the PR #89 review and the code.

- **Q-286 TSL port of the prototype's world (superseded by D-37; kept as the fallback).** Before D-37, stream D ported the approved prototype's rocky and water world to three.js r187 TSL: `viewer/renderers/RockyWorld.ts`, fed by the climate budget, terrain, rivers and world worker of Q-240 to Q-245, with the TSL orbit view (`viewer/orbit/`, Q-274). D-37 replaced it as the main renderer with the verbatim engine. Commit 49bfc63, which D-37 names, is not in this repository's history. **Status:** Superseded by D-37. Kept as the fallback (Atish, 11 Nov 2026), together with `GasWorld.ts` (Q-250 to Q-259): it draws at "+ Estimated" and "Measured only" (Q-287), and whenever the engine refuses, fails, loses its context or WebGL2 is missing (D-37). Owner: Moon.
- **Q-287 The engines draw only at "+ Artistic" (Decided by Atish, 11 Nov 2026).** As first built, the star, world and rock engines drew at "+ Estimated" or higher, each part keeping its tag (Q-299, Q-314; D-37 cited "Q-287 (visible at + Estimated)"). The PR #89 review found that this decided the honesty level in code, against D-21 (the start state shows only what data or a stated formula backs) and Q-110 (a [verify] constant only at its tier). **Decision:** the star, world and rock engines mount only at honesty `artistic` ("+ Artistic"). At "+ Estimated" (D-21's start state) and "Measured only" the three.js TSL path draws, with each layer at its own tag. This is consistent with D-21 and Q-110; D-21 is not amended. Built: `ViewerPanel.tsx` sets `enginesShown = honesty === 'artistic'`, which gates the star and world engines and the `RockStage` mount. It answers Moon's PR #89 question "Physics bodies are shown at the default + Estimated level (Q-287): confirm". The proposed build exception (Q-113 item 7, Q-200: Build it raises the level to + Artistic when the plan hides layers) is still open; if accepted, the engines show after Build it. Spec: [R5.3](../rendering/display-rules.md#r53-honesty-toggle-3-states), [R5.9](../rendering/engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37). Owner: Atish (D-21, Q-110). **Status:** Decided (Atish, 11 Nov 2026). **Amended by:** Q-367 (the `?engines=0` kill switch keeps the three.js path at every level, also at + Artistic).
- **Q-288 Ocean basin in the world adapter.** D-37 says Q-288 "still applies to the adapter"; reconstructed from the code. The TSL world model gives `planet_rocky` an ocean share of 0 (no basin, so Titan's lakes are not drawn, Q-285) and `planet_water` 0.7 (Q-240), and rivers and lakes need a basin (Q-244). The engine adapter (`cardToWorld`) follows the prototype instead: a `planet_water` card is an ocean world (sea 2, ocean fraction 1, as the prototype's K2-18 b); any other world gets the prototype's sea share 0.5 (`DEFAULT_SEA`), tagged `model_dependent` and listed as an assumption; a lava world gets no water sea (0, `inferred_from_analog`, Q-330); an artifact preset keeps its own sea. So a rocky exoplanet drawn by the engine has an ocean basin that the TSL fallback does not draw. Which rule is intended is open. Owner: Moon. **Status:** Open (built; Moon to confirm).

Q-303 to Q-306 (issue #71, stream W, the engine port of D-37):

- **Q-303 Engine layout and one canvas.** The engine lives in `frontend/src/engine/worlds/`. `helpers.js`, `shaders.js`, `presets.js`, `terrain.js`, `gas.js` and `core.js` are generated by `frontend/scripts/extract-world-engine.py` from `sculptor-preview-next.html` lines 1267-2346; each copied block sits between `// @verbatim Lstart-Lend` and `// @end-verbatim` and `parity/verbatim_check.py` proves it is byte-identical (renames declared). The prototype's globals (`W`, `EB`, `GS`, `OCEANF`, `ORB`) are kept as closure variables of `createCore(canvas)`, so every `WorldEngine` has its own state; only the DOM and UI lines were left out (the `$()` labels, pills, sliders, listeners). `WorldEngine` is one canvas and one GL context with the prototype's split viewport (fly view left 60%, globe right 40%, or the globe alone); `view()` gives the rectangles. Raw WebGL2, per D-37. **Amended by:** Q-335 (reviewed fixes marked `@deviation`; the extractor refuses to overwrite them; `parity/verbatim_check.py` is not in the repo).
- **Q-304 `cardToWorld` and the artifact table.** `cardToWorld(card, overrides)` returns `{ok, preset, overrides, tags, assumptions, missing, mismatches, artifact}` or `{ok:false, missing}`. For the objects the prototype already has (`ARTIFACT_PRESETS`: simbad-trappist-1e, -1f, simbad-kepler-186f, simbad-k2-18b, simbad-hd-189733b, ref-jupiter, ref-saturn, ref-titan, ref-earth) the prototype preset is used whole, seed included (Moon, 11 Nov 2026); a card value is kept (with its own tag) only when it equals the preset within 3%, otherwise `mismatches` lists it and the preset value is used. Any other card uses general defaults, each tagged `model_dependent` and listed: fluid water, 1 bar, mu 29 (18 for `planet_water`, 28 under methane, 2.3 for a giant), sea 0.5, eccentricity 0, obliquity 0, host the Sun for `ref-` bodies, orbital period by Kepler's third law when the card has none. Locking: a measured rotation period within 2% of the orbital period means locked, any other rotation period means not locked, and with no rotation period the app's rule (`deriveLocking`, A7.1) decides; a giant with neither refuses. Seed outside the table: FNV-1a of the card id, folded to 1..997. Live cards: on 11 Nov 2026 every non-demo exoplanet the backend resolves (Kepler-22 b, Proxima Cen b, TOI-700 d, HD 209458 b ...) has a thin `candidate` card (Q-282, archive outage), so `cardToWorld` refuses it with the missing list; a user's own full card is drawn by the same code. **Amended by:** Q-330 (a preset value keeps the card's tag only within 0.5 %; `ref-earth` removed; Titan's orbit; albedo from the card's temperature), Q-288 (sea share), Q-347 (the seed is the full 32-bit hash of the card id, with `seed3`), Q-359 (no albedo from `teq_K`).
- **Q-305 Parity proof.** `frontend/engine-parity.html` (dev only) renders a preset or a card; `frontend/scripts/engine-parity.cjs` drives it against the prototype. The orchestrator's `parity/run.cjs` drives the app through `window.__sxEngine` (`parity(case)`, `capture(size)`), installed by `src/engine/parity/hook.ts` only in a dev build with `?parity=1`; `main.tsx` has the one line that loads it. **Amended by:** Q-335 (`parity/run.cjs` is not in the repo; the scripts need `SX_PREVIEW_HTML` or `REF`), Q-329 (star type rows load with `?preset=`).
- **Q-306 Timing knobs not ported.** The prototype's 14 ms spin-up budget and frame-time resolution scaler are part of the verbatim `WorldGL`, so they stay; `WorldEngine.spinUp()` finishes the spin-up the way the prototype's test page does. Because the number of ticks depends on speed, `W.step` (how far the clouds have developed) differs between runs, in the prototype as well; `spinUp(dt, true)` makes it identical by advancing `performance.now()` 1 ms per call during the spin-up, and the artifact's driver must use the same clock (and the unwidened 14 ms / 40-step budget) to be comparable. The parity hook pins the render scale (`window.__sxPin`, a declared rename) and draws the globe before entering the fly view, as a user would. **Amended by:** Q-342 (the scaler aims at 30 fps and the world engine's pixel ratio is capped per role), Q-343 (at most 3 gas steps a frame).

## Q-299 to Q-302 (stream V3, rock engine, issue #71, D-37)

Status for all: **Open (built)**, Moon to confirm.

- **Q-299 The prototype's rock engine draws every asteroid and comet nucleus (D-37).** `frontend/src/engine/rocks/source.ts` is the prototype's `#rocks` code copied by `frontend/scripts/extract-rock-engine.py` (line slices of `sculptor-preview-next.html`: `RP`, `R`, `radialFn`, `rockModel`, `buildRock`, `ROCK_VS/FS`, `JET_VS/FS`, `RockGL`, the `NOISE` block, `rng`, `compile`, `unis`); nothing inside is edited, the file only has a header, one shim (`reduced`) and exports. `RockView` wraps what the prototype's page code did (rebuild, drag, loop). `RockStage` (build pane globe stage) and `RockCloseUp` (close-up pane) host it on raw WebGL2 canvases over the three.js viewer. It is drawn for `asteroid_procedural`, `asteroid_shape` and `comet` when the plan drew the body and the level is **+ Artistic** (Q-287, Atish, 11 Nov 2026; first built from "+ Estimated", which the review found against D-21): a generated surface is not measured, so "Measured only" and "+ Estimated" keep the three.js body. The relief and displacement layers of the three.js plan stay as they were. No WebGL2, no size on the card, or a failed engine leaves the three.js body (D-32, never blank).
- **Q-300 The card adapter and the artifact's own bodies.** `cardToRock(card)` fills the prototype's preset: size (`diameter_km`, or twice `nucleus_radius_km_max` for a comet), length to width `10^(0.4 A)` from the light curve (else round; a comet gets the prototype's 1.5 two-lobed nucleus, `artistic`), spin (missing: nothing forces solid rock), albedo (card, else the class analog 0.044 or 0.25 from Bennu and Eros, else 0.12, `inferred_from_analog`), density (class analog 1200 or 2670 kg/m3, generic 1500; comet 530; `inferred_from_analog`), rock strength 1 MPa and surface age 70 (the prototype's defaults inside `rockModel` and `R.age`, `artistic`). A missing size refuses. For the three bodies the artifact has as presets (`sbdb-50788063` 'Oumuamua, `sbdb-20101955` Bennu, `sbdb-20000433` Eros) the artifact's own values, tint and seed are used (an explicit id table), so the picture is 1:1; any other card uses its own values and `seed % 97` (the prototype's seeds are small because the shader adds the seed to a position). The colour is the card's when the plan allows one, else the prototype's dark rock tint. **Amended by:** Q-334 (Bennu density 1,190; the seed is hashed into [0, 97) in 1/4096 steps; preset badges say "the card says X"), Q-353 (a card body's noise takes `seed3` and its craters the full 32-bit hash).
- **Q-301 Shape models (Bennu, 67P) keep their mesh.** The mesh is turned into a radial height map (`meshRadial.ts`: each texel holds the farthest surface along that direction from the bounding-box centre; star-shaped, so a deep overhang is lost) and fed to the engine in place of the generated lumps and craters; the engine's ridges, boulders, Lommel-Seeliger lighting, cast shadows and (comet) jets still apply. `?rockmesh=off` draws the generated body instead (used to compare with the artifact). Jets are the prototype's `JET_VS/FS` points, strongest on the sunlit side; for a mesh comet they start on the engine's two-lobed outline, not on the mesh.
- **Q-302 Controls and notes in the `BuildControls` slot; known differences.** Pause, Surface age (0 to 100, rebuilds the craters in the engine; reduced motion starts paused), readouts with their badges (size, shape, spin, reflectivity, density, strength, gravity, relief it can hold, craters, likely structure) and the "How each part is worked out" notes (the prototype's five lines plus the values and where they came from). Drag or the arrow keys turn the body. Differences from the prototype: the engine draws at the device pixel ratio up to 1.5 (its own code; the app's 1.25 cap and quarter-scale-while-interacting are not applied inside the verbatim `draw`); the close-up pane shows the same framing at the pane's size, not a nearer camera; the light direction is the prototype's fixed one, not the card's Sun direction. Status: noted. **Amended by:** Q-334 (shape-model bodies hide the lump and crater readouts; the lighting is tagged `artistic`).

- **Q-309 Star engine hosted (D-37).** `StarEngine.ts` runs the four slices verbatim inside one function scope per instance (the slices are marked `@verbatim` for the parity check; the only edit is the declared `m.best=>(CLOSE_AT||m.best)` close-up target hook). Globe mode fills the build pane's stage and flare mode fills the left pane; both are raw WebGL2 on their own canvases. The TSL star path stays as the fallback when WebGL2 is missing or the card is refused, and at "+ Estimated" and "Measured only" (Q-287). **Amended by:** Q-329 (console errors forwarded; CSP note). Light defaults to True colour (the artifact defaults to Observatory view); firing a flare switches to Observatory view as the artifact does.
- **Q-310 `cardToStar` inputs.** Teff, radius, rotation from the card with the card's tag; radius and mass missing fall to the Mamajek main-sequence table (`model_dependent`), a giant gets 1 solar mass (`inferred_from_analog`), a missing rotation period uses the Sun's Rossby number through Wright's turnover time (`inferred_from_analog`). No Teff refuses (D-32), brown dwarfs and Miras are out of scope. The artifact's presets are used when the card is that star (`ARTIFACT_PRESET_BY_CARD_ID`: Sun, Procyon as 'f', eps Eri as 'k', Barnard's Star as 'm'); where the card disagrees by more than 1 % (Procyon 6589 K vs 6500 K, and so on) the preset wins, the value is tagged `inferred_from_analog` and the difference is listed under "Filled in, not measured". Moon to confirm. **Amended by:** Q-329 (only the Sun maps to a preset; Procyon, eps Eri and Barnard's Star draw from their cards), Q-358 (the turnover time is Reiners et al. 2022's, from the luminosity).
- **Q-311 Star close-up pick.** Clicking the star globe (or the arrow keys while it has focus) sets the left close-up's target through `useStarTarget`; the left pane then shows the artifact's `Close-up` view there, via the same engine with the target override. Limb and space do nothing; "Back to the active region" returns to `m.best`. The artifact has no overlays in its close view, so loops and plasma are not drawn there.
- **Q-312 Mass is not on star cards.** Stellar mass is always an assumption (Q-310), so the Rossby number and field are `model_dependent`; fix by adding a mass field to the card contract (open). **Amended by:** Q-363 (`mass_msun` and `lum_lsun` from Gaia FLAME on star cards, `model_dependent`; Barnard's Star and Procyon have no FLAME mass).

## Q-313 to Q-316 (stream Y, world engine hosted in the app, issue #71, D-37)

Status for all: **Open (built)**, Moon to confirm.

- **Q-313 Two engine instances, one per canvas.** The prototype draws the globe and the fly view on one canvas (fly 60 %, globe 40 %). The app has them in two panes (globe in the build pane, fly view in the left close-up pane), so a planet is two `WorldEngine` instances: the globe on the build pane's stage (fly off, the globe fills its canvas) and the fly view in the left pane (fly on; its canvas is 1/0.6 as wide as the pane so the left 60 % is the pane and the globe half is cropped away). One instance spanning both panes was rejected: the engine fixes the split at 60/40 of its own canvas, which cannot line up with two separate panes, and copying rectangles between canvases would resample every frame (no longer pixel for pixel). Cost: two GL contexts and two simulations per planet. Terrain, climate and rivers are seeded and identical; the cloud and gas-flow fields run under the prototype's 14 ms spin-up budget, so a spin-up that is not finished the same way on both leaves the cloud placement slightly different between the two canvases (the same variation the prototype has between machines, Q-306). `worldStore` applies every control change to both, remembers what was applied (a close-up that opens later replays it and copies the turn angle), and carries the globe click (`globePick`) to the fly view, which goes there as the prototype does (position only; height, heading and look stay). Entry draws the globe view once, then `setFly(true)`, which lands via `flyAimPeak` (rocky) or `flyAimDefault`.
- **Q-314 Hosting rule, controls and notes.** `WorldGlobeStage` and `WorldFlyStage` (`app/WorldEngineStage.tsx`) draw planet_rocky, planet_water and gas_giant cards that `cardToWorld` accepts; the old ShaderBody, RockyWorld, GasWorld and the TSL orbit view stay for a card it refuses, a browser without WebGL 2 or float targets, an engine that fails to start or loses its context, and "Measured only" and "+ Estimated" (a simulated surface is not measured, the same rule as the star and rock engines; "+ Estimated" added by Q-287, Atish, 11 Nov 2026). While the world spins up an opaque "Forming the planet…" (gas: "Forming the atmosphere", the artifact's wording) covers the canvas, so a half-formed world is never shown; the prototype itself draws a rocky world while it forms and hides only a gas giant, so this is a deliberate difference. The Details panel gets every artifact control (Surface fluid, Pause, Time for a giant, Spin, Crust, Show, CO₂ thermostat, Clouds, Zoom, Axial tilt, Orbit eccentricity, Star temperature, Orbit size, Atmosphere pressure) mapped onto `WorldEngine.setOverrides`, the engine's readout rows with a badge each, and the "How each part is worked out" notes with the artifact's honesty line. What `cardToWorld` filled in is the readout "Filled in, not measured" and a second note listing every assumption, any card value that differs from the artifact preset, and the optional fields that were absent. The viewer's own Pause and gas-pacing buttons are hidden while the engine draws. The old orbit-view controls (height slider, Fly forward, What is drawn) are reproduced on the fly pane with the artifact's scroll (`altRange`), drag, arrows and W A S D.
- **Q-315 Haptics read the engine's own arrays.** `WorldFieldSampler` (`engine/worlds/fieldSampler.ts`) wraps `WorldEngine.cpuFields()` (terrain elevation, climate temperature, rain and land mask) and has the same `sampleUV` / `sampleDirection` returning a `FieldSample` as the old `FieldSampler`, so the haptic sources need no change; height is the terrain height scaled to the highest ground, `ocean` is the climate land mask, and `band`, `storm`, `lava` and `ice` read 0 (the engine has no such channel; they are listed in `missing`). `worldCpuFields()` in `worldStore` gives the sources the arrays of the world on screen. The Feel panel is still not wired to the 3D view (stream U), so nothing calls this yet.
- **Q-316 Known differences from the artifact.** (1) A rocky world is covered until it has spun up (Q-314). (2) With the close-up open the globe does not turn on drag: a click goes to the fly view, as the artifact does in fly mode; with it closed a rocky world turns by drag or arrow keys. (3) The fly pane has no height-to-"Surface view" toggle: it opens with the build, as the mock-up's close-up does. (4) The render scale drops on slow frames and DPR is capped at 1.5, both as in the artifact; `window.__sxPin` pins it (parity and screenshots only). **Amended by:** Q-344 (with the close-up open a click picks and a drag turns the globe; item 2), Q-342 (pixel ratio 1.0 fly, 1.25 globe; item 4).
