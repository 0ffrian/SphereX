## Q-329 to Q-335 PR #89 review fixes to the engines (11 Nov 2026)

Status for all: **Open (built)**, Moon to confirm, except where an entry names Atish's call. Engine changes inside copied code are declared `@deviation` blocks or `@rename` pairs (Q-335). Q-332 and Q-333 are in [rendering-kinds](rendering-kinds.md).

- **Q-329 Star engine fixes (amends Q-309, Q-310; corrects Q-234 and Q-270).** (1) **Presets:** only `ref-sun` maps to an artifact preset (row `g`). Procyon, eps Eri and Barnard's Star draw from their own card values through the tier chain: the type rows had overruled their cards (eps Eri drawn at 4,500 K and 30 d, Procyon at 1.3 R☉ and 8 d). A card with no rotation period gets the Sun's Rossby number times Wright's turnover time, `inferred_from_analog` (**amended by** Q-358: Reiners et al. 2022's turnover time). The prototype's type rows stay in `PROTOTYPE_PRESETS` for the parity harness only, loaded with `?preset=`. (2) **Flare close-up note:** the close-up replays a scripted event on a Sun-like star (5,772 K) with one fixed spot pair, whichever star is chosen; the note says so and no longer claims a potential-field model. (3) **Limb law (deviation):** the Eddington approximation T⁴ = ¾ Teff⁴ (μ + ⅔), so the disc centre is 1.0574 Teff; the brightness is divided by 1.0574^β so the centre intensity is unchanged. `model_dependent`. The prototype's `0.4 + 0.6μ` drew every star as if at 0.946 Teff. (4) **Spot temperature (deviation):** the umbra is cooler than the surface by dT = clamp(350 + 0.6 (Teff − 3300), 200, 2000) K, a linear fit to the trend in Berdyugina 2005 (Living Rev. Sol. Phys. 2, 8), the coefficients **[verify]**; `inferred_from_analog`. The Sun's umbra stays near 3,939 K; the prototype's fixed 0.68 Teff over-darkened M-dwarf spots. (5) **Granulation contrast** (±12 % in temperature) is labelled `artistic`: two to three times the observed contrast. (6) **X10 readout (deviation):** about once per 900 days for the Sun (GOES since 1976: about one flare of X10 or more per 800 to 1,000 days), `inferred_from_analog`; the prototype's rate was 2.5 to 3 times too high. (7) **Butterfly (corrects Q-234):** the TSL path's belt swings from about 22° to 8.4° around 15.3°, not 30° to 8° (the observed butterfly starts nearer 30°); the star engine has a fixed belt with no drift, and its note says so. (8) **Citations (corrects Q-270):** the energy cap is the budget of Shibata et al. 2013, PASJ 65, 49 (not "Shibata and Yokoyama"); GOES class energies are a convention (Emslie et al. 2012, ApJ 759, 71). (9) **Errors:** the slices' `console.error` and `console.warn` reach the real console (tagged `[star engine]`); their `console.log` is dropped. **Known limitation:** the slices run through `new Function`, so a CSP needs `'unsafe-eval'`. Deviations (3), (4) and (6) were Atish's call on the review (header of `engine/stars/protoStar1.js`). Spec: [R7.5](../rendering/renderers/stars.md#r75-stars-star-star_mira-star_ttauri). Owner: Moon.
- **Q-330 World engine and `cardToWorld` fixes (amends Q-241, Q-280, Q-304, Q-319).** (1) **Clouds:** the water cloud-base mixing ratio is 0.01 in the engine too (`presets.js`, outside the verbatim block), so Earth's cloud base is about 0.72 km instead of 0 km. The fly view's cloud deck now reads `uCloudKm` from the model's cloud base (at least 0.3 km; 3.5 km when nothing condenses, and then no deck is drawn); it was a fixed 3 km. (2) **CO₂ can be liquid:** CO₂'s 1 atm point is a sublimation point (194.7 K, below its triple point), so the boiling test never gave a liquid. When Tb < Tt the liquid–vapour line is drawn up from the triple point by Clausius–Clapeyron with Lv about 15.3 kJ/mol (NIST Chemistry WebBook, **[verify]**), capped at the critical point 304.13 K and 7.3773 MPa (Span and Wagner 1996) (**amended by** Q-355: every fluid now has a critical point; Q-356: `Lv` also sets the saturation pressure above the triple point). Other fluids are unchanged; `physics/fluids.ts` uses the same rule. (3) **Lava worlds (amends Q-319):** a rocky world with no pressure on its card and Teq of at least 1,400 K (the basalt solidus, the engine's `LAVA.Tsol`) gets a thin rock-vapour atmosphere: 0.005 bar, mu 44, no sea (the artifact's own lava-world example), `inferred_from_analog` (millibar rock-vapour atmospheres: Schaefer and Fegley 2009; Ito et al. 2015, **[verify]**). 55 Cnc e is now drawn airless with its magma showing, not as a steam world, and its title comes from the card name. (4) **Card against preset (amends Q-304):** a preset value keeps the card's tag only within 0.5 % (was 3 %); otherwise it is the artifact's setting, tagged `inferred_from_analog` with "the card says X" (Titan's 1.5 bar against the measured 1.467). (5) **Titan (amends Q-280):** one orbit, the heliocentric one: P is Saturn's year by Kepler's third law (`derived`); Titan's 15.95-day orbit around Saturn is its spin. (6) The `ref-earth` mapping is removed (no backend object). (7) **Albedo:** the Bond albedo is inverted from the card's surface temperature or `teq_K` so the engine's Teq (and its grey greenhouse) match the card (`derived`); else the card's albedo (`model_dependent`, with a note that a geometric albedo is not a Bond albedo); else the default 0.3, listed as an assumption (**amended by** Q-359: `teq_K` is no longer inverted; a measured surface temperature still is). Spec: [R5.9](../rendering/engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37). Owner: Moon.
- **Q-331 Gas giant fixes: rings, winds, flattening, storms (amends Q-252, Q-253, Q-254).** (1) **Saturn's rings:** in the engine the Encke Gap is centred at 2.2165 R_S (133,584 km) with half-width 0.0027 R_S (the gap is about 325 km wide), and the F ring half-width is 0.0012 R_S (one texel at 2.326 R_S, so it stays visible). In the TSL fallback the F ring is 140,180 to 140,240 km and `ENCKE_GAP_KM` is 133,584, from the PDS Ring-Moon Systems Node's Saturn ring table, **[verify]**; the fact sheet's 139,826 and 133,410 km (Q-252) are older values. `classifyRing` uses the named gaps (Encke Gap, Cassini Division) for the haptics. (2) **Winds:** a reference giant's measured peak jet replaces the thermal-wind estimate: Jupiter about 150 m/s (the 24° N jet), Saturn about 450 m/s (equatorial), `measured`, **[verify]** (recalled, not re-opened). Other giants keep U = 0.6 √(R dT), whose 10 K floor gives about 115 to 120 m/s for any cool unlocked giant: an estimate, `model_dependent`. Saturn's Rhines jet count drops from 13 to 7. (3) **Step length (corrects Q-254):** one solver step is about 1,525 s of planet time for Jupiter and about 430 s for Saturn, not "about 38 minutes". (4) **Flattening:** measured for Jupiter (0.06487) and Saturn (0.09796), from the NASA fact sheets, `measured`; other giants keep f = 0.7 q, `model_dependent` (it gives Saturn 0.108). The ring and planet shadows use the flattened shape. The backend's rule differs (Q-332). (5) **Storms:** the Great Red Spot is r 0.083 planet radii with aspect 1.49 (about 11,000 × 16,350 km, Simon et al. 2024, PSJ 5, 223); cyclones (type 2, the polar clusters) now spin cyclonically; band latitudes are planetographic; on non-reference giants the storm size comes from the deformation radius (TSL path). (6) **Citations:** Jupiter's internal-heat ratio 1.67 cites Hanel et al. 1981 (Li et al. 2018 revised it to about 2.1); Saturn's 77 K is the low end of Hanel et al. 1983. Spec: [R7.7](../rendering/renderers/shader-kinds.md#r77-shader-generated-kinds-planet_rocky-planet_water-gas_giant-nebula-nova) (the TSL path), [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values) (the engine's values), [R5.9](../rendering/engines.md#r59-prototype-engines-d-37). Owner: Moon.
- **Q-334 Rock engine fixes (amends Q-300, Q-302; Q-210 item 5).** (1) **Bennu's density** is 1,190 kg/m³ (Lauretta et al. 2019) everywhere (`RHO_C`); the prototype's own rubble example keeps its rounded 1,200 inside the verbatim code (within 1 %, no mismatch noted). (2) **Seed:** a card's 32-bit seed is hashed (murmur3 finaliser) into [0, 97) in steps of 1/4096, about 400,000 values instead of the 97 of `seed % 97`; the artifact's preset bodies keep their integer seeds (**amended by** Q-353: a card body's noise takes `seed3` and its crater RNG the full hash). (3) **Preset bodies:** where the drawn preset differs from the card, the badge says "the card says X". (4) **Shape-model bodies** (Bennu, 67P) hide the lump and crater readouts, which describe a field that is not drawn. (5) **Lighting:** a Lommel–Seeliger and Lambert blend with an exponential phase darkening and no opposition surge, not fitted to any body: `artistic`. (6) **Physics:** `extract-rock-engine.py` applies PATCHES that mirror the baker (Q-326): crater depth 0.33 r, the reachable crater cap 0.114, rubble relief at the angle of repose. (7) **Jets are built** (Q-301), so Q-210 item 5's "not built yet" no longer holds. Spec: [R7.2](../rendering/renderers/asteroid.md#r72-asteroid_procedural). Owner: Moon.
