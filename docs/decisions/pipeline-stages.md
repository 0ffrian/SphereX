| Q-63 | Exoplanet Archive adapter and type rule (issue #40, items 4 and 5); implements Q-56, signed off by Moon (issue #68; D-17 amended). **Rule (option b, mass and radius together)**, central values only (error bars ignored); every number is in `PROPOSED_EXOPLANET_THRESHOLDS` in `backend/app/core/classify/rules.py` and is still **[verify]**. **On since issue #68 Part 0.1 (signed off by Moon, 9 Nov 2026; D-17 amended):** the live `EXOPLANET_THRESHOLDS` = `dict(PROPOSED_EXOPLANET_THRESHOLDS)`, with `ASSET_VERSION` 5. Before that they were all `None` (PR #45 review), so every archive planet was `candidate` with `type:exoplanet_thresholds`; a value set back to `None` still refuses that way. **Checked against the sources (astro-verifier, issue #68 comment):** 0.414 M_J (Chen & Kipping 2017, Table 1, T(2)) and 317.83 (IAU 2015 B3) are as published; 1.5 R_E (Weiss & Marcy 2014; Fulton et al. 2017) and 6 R_E (Kopparapu et al. 2018) are project cuts based on those papers, which do not define them as class boundaries; 8 points of the Zeng 2019 Earth-like table match. **Known inconsistency, kept:** the radius cut (≥ 6 R_E) and the mass cut (≥ 0.414 M_J, about 10-12 R_E in Chen & Kipping's Fig. 3) do not select the same planets, so a 6-10 R_E planet is a gas giant by radius but Neptunian by mass; no demo planet is in that range. Live classes: TRAPPIST-1 e, TRAPPIST-1 f and Kepler-186 f `planet_rocky`, K2-18 b `planet_water`, HD 189733 b `gas_giant`. A card refused on its type (`type:*` in `missing`) is never saved as an asset, so no refusal outlives the decision. R = `pl_rade`; M = `pl_bmasse` only when `pl_bmassprov` is `Mass`. **Archive calculated values (PR #45 review):** PSCompPars fills gaps with its own estimates and marks them `refstr=CALCULATED_VALUE` in `<column>_reflink` (https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/pscp_calc.html): a radius from the Chen & Kipping 2017 mass-radius relation (often from an Msini mass), a mass from the radius, Teq = T★ · sqrt(R★/a) · (1/4)^(1/4). A calculated `pl_rade` or `pl_bmasse` counts as missing for the rule (the same circularity as `M-R relationship`); on 2026-11-07, 1,540 rows had a calculated `pl_rade` and 1,090 a calculated `pl_eqt`. A calculated `pl_eqt` still picks the water curve (it is derived from measured stellar and orbital values). Fixtures: 51 Peg b (calculated R 14.1, true mass) -> `gas_giant` by mass; Proxima Cen b (calculated R 1.02, Msini) -> `candidate` `type:exoplanet_radius`. (1) R >= 6 R⊕: `gas_giant` (Kopparapu et al. 2018, arXiv:1802.09602). (2) R missing and true M >= 0.414 M_J (Chen & Kipping 2017, arXiv:1603.08614, Table 1: 0.414 +0.057/-0.065 M_J) = 131.6 M⊕ using 317.83 M⊕ per M_J (ratio of the IAU 2015 B3 nominal GM_J/GM_E, arXiv:1510.07674): `gas_giant`. (3) R < 1.5 R⊕: `planet_rocky` (Weiss & Marcy 2014, arXiv:1312.0936; Fulton et al. 2017, arXiv:1703.10375). (4) 1.5 <= R < 6 needs a true mass, else `candidate` with `type:exoplanet_mass`. With M, R is compared with Zeng et al. 2019 (PNAS 116, 9723, arXiv:1906.04253) curves from https://lweb.cfa.harvard.edu/~lzeng/planetmodels.html (tables copied in `zeng2019.py`, retrieved 2026-11-07, R linear in ln M, no extrapolation): R <= Earth-like rocky (32.5% Fe + 67.5% MgSiO3) -> `planet_rocky`; above it and <= the 100% H2O curve -> `planet_water`; the water curve is the tabulated temperature (300, 500, 700 or 1000 K; 1 mbar radius) nearest `pl_eqt`; R above the water curve -> `candidate` `type:exoplanet_envelope` (too large for rock plus water: likely a H/He envelope). In the water band with `pl_eqt` missing or above 1000 K -> `candidate` `type:exoplanet_water_model_range` (no tabulated water model; this stops the 300 K curve calling 55 Cnc e, 1958 K, a water world). Mass outside a curve's table -> `type:exoplanet_mass_range`; R missing and no giant mass -> `type:exoplanet_radius`. **Mass provenance:** live `select distinct pl_bmassprov` gives `Mass` (2491 rows), `Msini` (926), `Msin(i)/sin(i)` (15), `M-R relationship` (3032). Only `Mass` is a mass: `Msini` is a lower limit, `M-R relationship` is the archive's own estimate from the radius (circular), and `Msin(i)/sin(i)` is left out as a conservative choice. **Results (archive values, 2026-11-07):** TRAPPIST-1 e (R 0.92) rocky; K2-18 b (2.37, 8.92 M⊕, 284 K) water; Kepler-22 b (2.10, 9.10, 279 K) water; Kepler-138 d (1.51, 2.10, 345 K) water, and it sits just above the 1.5 cut, so it is threshold-sensitive; HD 209458 b (15.58) gas giant; GJ 1214 b (2.73, 8.41, 567 K) candidate (envelope); 55 Cnc e (1.875, 7.99, 1958 K) candidate (water model range). **`planet_water` is a model result** (the interior is not observed), but the card has no field to carry that tag: propose a field such as `interior_model` tagged `model_dependent` with the assumption. **Adapter:** `pscomppars` by `lower(pl_name)` through `https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/TAP/sync` (`SX_EXOPLANET_TAP_URL`), tested live 2026-11-07, D-25 cache `exoplanet_archive`; names tried: the query, the SIMBAD main_id, each with a space before a trailing planet letter; SIMBAD otype `Pl` only (not `Pl?`). **Card:** `radius_rjup` = `pl_radj` (`measured`, with the paper; `model_dependent` with the mass-radius assumption when the archive calculated it), `teq_K` = `pl_eqt` (`measured`, D-12; `derived` with the archive formula when calculated) else null/`derived`.
