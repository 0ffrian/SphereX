## Notes

**Q-107 fixed, items (2) and (3):** the water world draws clouds as a minority over an ocean (a flatter cover-to-threshold mapping and 0.85 opacity when the field has a sea), a thinner disc haze there, a slightly rougher sea so the Sun glint is a spot, and the artist default sea level is 0.58 (was 0.4), so the ocean reads blue as in water_world.jpg; ice from the A7.1 Teq thresholds and all data-derived values are unchanged. The star's granulation is finer and about a third of the contrast (GRAIN_AMP 0.045, MOTTLE_AMP 0.02, thinner dark lanes), and the limb is crisp: a short steeper darkening in the last 30% of mu on top of the linear law, and a tighter, fainter glow that starts at the limb. The prominence loop shape was left as is at that point (it arches on the real WebGPU backend).

**Q-107 fixed after the PR #52 self-review (items 1 and 3, and the water world again):** (1) the terminator is Minnaert-softened for gas giants only (`MINNAERT_K` 0.8 in `renderers/jetSurface.ts`, artistic): a softer terminator and a slightly darker limb; airless rock keeps its sharp terminator. This deviates from the textbook Lambert surface and the deviation is documented in the code. (2) The water-world atmosphere haze is reduced (thinner shell, `ATMO_SHELL` 1.04 in `renderers/ShaderBody.ts`, less haze over the disc), after the review found that the earlier haze lit the night side and greyed the ocean. (3) Granulation and limb were fixed as above; prominence strands now taper, differ in height and phase, and fade over the disc. The star tint and the brown-dwarf jet palette are now converted from sRGB once (`renderers/linearColour.ts`). **Still open:** (4) the brown dwarf in its default SPHEREx IR view shows no motion by design (the jets are drawn in the visible view only, and with Q-108 no process note is shown there); a UI hint such as "Switch to the visible view to see the jets move" was an idea at that point and is built in issue #56 (see the next paragraph); and none of the visual changes has been looked at on a real GPU (the goldens come from the test backend) **[verify on a real GPU]**; frame times on the Iris Xe are still not measured (Q-104 item 10).

**Q-107 item (1) fixed (PR #63 review):** the straight terminator was a Sun exactly in the image plane (the fixtures' `sun_vector` `[1, 0, 0]` read as scene space, Q-62) plus an infinite slope of the gas Minnaert term at the terminator; the bare `sun_dir` hint is now tilted toward the camera (`SUN_HINT_DISPLAY_TILT`, artistic, not for comets), the Minnaert `mu0` is floored (`MINNAERT_MU0_FLOOR` 0.3) and a faint `GAS_NIGHT_FLOOR` 0.012 keeps the night limb readable (R5.1).

**Q-107 item (4) done (issue #56):** when a brown dwarf is in the SPHEREx infrared view and its `jets` layer is missing with reason `mode` (the visible view would draw it, so only at a honesty level that shows artistic layers and with a stated winds speed-up and the methane depth), the viewer panel shows "Switch to "Visible colour" to see the jets move. This motion is an artist’s impression." in a polite status region (`ir-jets-hint`) directly under the mode buttons, linked to the Visible colour button with `aria-describedby` (`app/ViewerPanel.tsx`); it shows only when a body is drawn. Nothing shows when the jets are hidden by honesty or refused for missing data, since the visible view would not draw them either. The wording went through the ux-microcopy review (verb "Switch to" as in the mode announcement, the artist's-impression note says it is the motion that is artistic, D-02 `artistic`). It names the mode button as labelled, "Visible colour". A mode or level change can queue the hint after the existing announcer's message; both are polite, so neither interrupts.
