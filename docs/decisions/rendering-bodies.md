# Decisions: Rendering: bodies and surfaces

> **Part of:** the decision log ([index](README.md)) · **Holds:** where surfaces are generated, the baker, renderers per type, physics sliders, accuracy, golden images · **Authority:** highest; an entry here wins over [rendering/](../rendering/README.md) and [architecture/](../architecture/README.md) (see the index for precedence)

## D-01 Where surfaces are generated

- **Context:** The architecture spec (§6) generated every body in the browser with shaders and triplanar mapping. The rendering doc (§5.5, §6) bakes height, normal and albedo maps on the backend in Python, as equirectangular images.
- **Decision:**
  - Every type defined in the rendering doc (`asteroid_shape`, `asteroid_procedural`, `comet`, `brown_dwarf`, `brown_dwarf_binary`, `star`, `star_mira`, `star_ttauri`, `candidate`) follows the rendering doc. Procedural maps are **baked by the backend**, never computed per frame.
  - The kinds that exist only in the architecture spec (`planet_rocky`, `planet_water`, `gas_giant`, `nebula`, `nova`; see D-04) keep the **browser shader generators** with triplanar mapping. Their haptics come from a shared CPU field (D-12).
- **Why:** Rendering doc principle 6 ("one height map drives everything") needs a height field that the finger, the normals and the sound can all sample. A shader-only surface does not provide one.
- **Amended by:** Q-47 (only `asteroid_procedural`, `brown_dwarf` and `brown_dwarf_binary` are baked), Q-103 (comet, nebula and nova drawn as volumes) and Q-104 (stars and brown-dwarf jets drawn in the browser); as-built table: [A6](../architecture/generators.md#a6-generators). To confirm as amendments: all three rows are open.

## D-26 Sliders on baked bodies (answers Q-03)

- **Decision:** For the event, baked types allow only **colour, lighting and spin** sliders. Relief sliders exist only on the shader kinds, which redraw live. A re-bake on every drag would be too slow.

## D-33 Accuracy first

> From the block "7 Nov 2026: tiers, accuracy and motion (issue #38)": Source: the team plan "Physics-backed Sky Sculptor" (6 Nov 2026). D-32 to D-34 were proposed by Moon as M2 owner (D-30). D-32 is accepted (Atish, PR #44); D-33 and D-34 are **pending team confirmation**.

- **Context:** how close a body can be to the real thing depends on what exists for it. The page must say which row a body is in.
- **Decision:**

  | What exists | What the user sees | Limit |
  |---|---|---|
  | Spacecraft shape model (Eros, Bennu, Ryugu, Itokawa, Vesta, 67P **[verify]** list) | The real shape, including large craters | Imperfect only below the model resolution. Source: NASA PDS Small Bodies Node (for example the Gaskell Eros Shape Model Bundle V1.1 from NEAR images, DOI 10.26033/d0gq-9427, verified 7 Nov 2026; licence CC0-1.0 per PR #45, **[verify]** until #45 is merged) |
  | Light-curve inversion model (DAMIT, each model graded for quality; scale **[verify]**: sources disagree) | The real overall shape, spin and pole | Convex: no craters or hollows (A3.7) |
  | Catalogue numbers only | Right size, spin and stretch | Procedural surface |
  | Unresolved star | Right colour, size and variability | Surface is physics-consistent, never imaged |
  | Exoplanet | Physically plausible | Never imaged |

  - A measured whole-body model is drawn as is (tier 1). Generation fills only detail **below its resolution** and never changes the silhouette.
  - A DAMIT convex model counts as tier 1 for the overall shape. Concavities must not be presented as measured: any crater or hollow drawn on it is tier 4 or 5.
  - The citation and licence travel on the card: the `shape_source` value carries the model data set, DOI and licence in its `source` (R3.2). `Layer` has no `source` field.
  - Minimum DAMIT quality grade to accept: **[verify]**.
- **Related:** A3.7, R7.1, Q-43, Q-44, Q-55 (4) (`SHAPE_MODELS` is empty today).
- **Status:** Pending team confirmation. Proposed by Moon on 7 Nov 2026; asked in PR #44.
- **Status note (9 Nov 2026):** the "[verify] until #45 is merged" caveat is out of date (PR #45 merged in c9c6173), and `SHAPE_MODELS` is no longer empty: the Gaskell Eros model is registered (Q-61).

## D-36 Physics-based realism for generated bodies (issue #71)

- **Context:** Moon reviewed a working prototype of every generated body on 10 Nov 2026 and asked for it to be built into the app. It changes how asteroids, comets, stars, flares, planets and gas giants are generated, and adds a score for how much of a picture is data. Reference: [Sculptor Realism Preview](https://claude.ai/artifact/U17UTpmCk9ma2srPhCy41R).
- **Decision:**
  - **Asteroids and comet nuclei are rocky by default.** Only data (a shape model, radar roughness, thermal inertia) makes a body smoother. Measured shape models are never altered.
  - **Data confidence** is `C = sum(w c) / sum(w)`: `c` is how much the data narrows the class range, `w` how much that quantity changes the picture. It adds to the 3 badges of D-02 and does not replace them. The panel states that it is not the probability the picture is correct.
  - **A 3D body needs** a known class, at least one key measurement for its type, and `C` at or above a threshold (0.25 to start, calibrated on the demo objects). Otherwise the page says what was found and what is needed.
  - **Stars, flares, planets and gas giants** use the physics in issue #71: spot and flare models from the magnetic field, a heat, ice and water budget per map cell, plate tectonics, fluid phases per point, and a vorticity solver over fixed deep jets.
  - **Plate tectonics, surface fluid and surface pressure are assumptions** for any exoplanet and are labelled so.
  - **Jupiter and Saturn are reference objects** with observed bands, named storms and Saturn's rings.
  - **A modelled comet tail is allowed if labelled.** This amends R7.3, which allowed a tail only from a published result.
  - **Simulations are deterministic:** state is a function of card, seed and step count, never wall-clock time or `Math.random`.
  - **Unchanged:** the card never guesses (`value: null` stays, D-32), and the scale bar needs a real size (Q-33).
- **Why:** the viewer should look real and still say plainly what is measured and what is assumed.
- **Status:** Decided by Moon, 10 Nov 2026 (3D rules, D-17). Atish reviews stage 7, the baker and haptics; Kevin reviews stage 6 and data sources.
