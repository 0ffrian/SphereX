| Q-115 | **Gas giant: five tiered layers instead of one artistic `surface` (issue #62 part B; D-21, D-32, A7.2, R7.7).** The single `surface` layer (tag `artistic`) hid everything below "+ Artistic", although the band count, the flattening and the colour follow A7.2 rules. The backend (`card_to_params._gas_giant_layers`, `app/core/render/gas_giant.py`) now sends `bands`, `flattening`, `colour`, `storms`, `turbulence`: (a) `bands`: Rhines count `N ~ sqrt(2 Omega R / U)`. The card has no wind field, so the typical wind is always an input: the best tag is **`model_dependent`** (not `derived` as the issue's first draft said: D-32 keeps `derived` for formulas whose inputs are all measured), with `assumed` naming `zonal_wind_ms`. A weaker spin tag gives a weaker layer, never a stronger one. **No `rot_period_h` on the card (Q-119 b, PR #63 review):** the typical spin stands in, which is "typical, no formula": the layer is **`inferred_from_analog`** (tier 4) with `assumed` naming `rot_period_h` (the typical value) and `zonal_wind_ms`; the legend and the viewer rule (`rhinesBands`, `flatteningRule`) say the same. (b) `flattening`: `f = f_J (P_J / P)^2` (Jupiter density assumed, the mass is on no card), `model_dependent` with a measured spin (`assumed` mean density); with no spin the typical spin equals Jupiter's period, so the value is Jupiter's own: `inferred_from_analog` (tier 4), `assumed` mean density and the typical spin. (c) `colour`: palette family from `teq_K`, `derived` (weakest of `derived` and the Teq tag); without `teq_K` it is `artistic` (the artist default) and still offered. (d) `storms`: **`artistic`** (PR #63 review: how many storms and where is all seeded, so it is a look, not an analogue). (e) `turbulence`: `artistic`. Typical values `TYPICAL_SPIN_H` 9.925 h and `TYPICAL_ZONAL_WIND_MS` 50 m/s (A7.2's Jupiter example), and Jupiter's period `JUPITER_SPIN_H` of the flattening rule (separate, so withdrawing the typical spin does not refuse a measured-spin flattening), are **[verify]** config constants, mirrored in the frontend (`shaderSpec.ts`); `null` refuses the layer. The viewer gives each layer its own switch (`ShaderSpec.gasLayers`, `plan.shader.gas`, `ShaderBody.setGasLayers`, `JetLayers` uniforms): at **Measured only** a plain sphere of the measured radius (nothing about the body is measured), at **+ Estimated** bands, flattening and colour, at **+ Artistic** the storms and the turbulence too. The "plain grey" note is dropped when the `colour` layer is drawn, and the Measured-only texts say "choose + Estimated" for the bands, flattening and colour and "+ Artistic" for the storms. The viewer's own `jets` and `storm_drift` (renamed from the viewer's `storms`, which clashed with the new backend id) stay `artistic`; `band_count` is replaced by `bands`. With no spin on the card the flattening and the bands use the typical spin at tier 4 (Q-119 b), instead of the old default 12 bands. The #60 mould steps for a gas giant are Base mould, Bands (bands, flattening, jets, turbulence), Storms, Colour. Rings stay a slider, not a layer. | Moon; backend half (stage 7 `card_to_params`, `gas_giant.py`, baker `bake_fixtures.py`) is Atish's per D-17 and he reviews it, not Kevin | Accepted by Atish (PR #63 approval, 9 Nov 2026); implemented as described. **Amends Q-109** (flattening with a measured spin at tier 3; with no spin it stays at tier 4 as Q-109 had it). PR #63 review: `ASSET_VERSION` is now **4** (stage 7 sends different layers; `CARD_TO_PARAMS_VERSION` stays "1" so seeds are stable). **Legend rule (PR #63 review):** the legend's `bands` and `flattening` take the weaker of the backend layer's tag and the viewer rule that sets the drawn value (`dynamics` rates, slider overrides), with that rule's text, formula and assumption; a slider shows `artistic`; a layer the backend did not send is refused with reason `backend`. |
