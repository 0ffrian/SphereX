| Q-71 | Pulse trains are owned by the engine (issue #9; R8.3, R8.8). R8.8's reference driver re-issues `emit({kind: 'pulseTrain'})` every 250 ms with `n = floor(250 / period)` pulses, so any rate below 4 Hz plays one pulse per window (the 1–2 Hz ocean swell of R8.5 plays at 4 Hz), and every re-issue restarts the phase. Implemented instead: `HapticEngine.startTrain(id, {rateHz, strength, tag, pitch?, pan?, voice?})`, `updateTrain` and `stopTrain`. The engine keeps each train's phase and sends a `TrainWindow` with explicit pulse times every ≤ 250 ms (still re-issued, as R8.3 asks); updates apply from the next window; one train at a time. A one-shot (tick) cancels the vibration pattern, as `navigator.vibrate` does; the engine re-sends the cancelled pulses to vibration only once it ends, so the train resumes on its phase. A train window counts as one event for the 40/s cap (a window that only re-sends cancelled pulses is not counted again), so the motor can start more than 40 pulses a second: the worst case is a 40 Hz train (`TRAIN_MAX_RATE_HZ`) plus 36 ticks, about 76 onsets a second, more if the one-shots are doubles. Atish to confirm that the cap counts events, not motor onsets. A timer that fires late plays the due pulse at once but keeps the grid, so lateness never accumulates. `stopTrain` (and a new train replacing the old one) cancels only the train's own output: a one-shot that is playing, such as a firm coastline tick or a ringing chime, is left alone. `emit(pulseTrain)` is kept and plays a single window, replacing (and silencing) any running train. Confirm, or amend R8.8. | Atish | Open (implemented as described); amended by D-39 (audio trains only) |

| Q-74 | Calibration choices (issue #10; R8.3, R8.7, D-09, D-13). (1) **Strictly increasing levels, capped at R8.3's 25 ms** (PR #36 review): firm = 25 ms always; soft = `minMs` (at most 23 ms, so three ticks fit under the cap); medium = R8.3's 15 ms, raised to soft + `CALIBRATION_LEVEL_STEP_MS` (5 ms), or halfway between soft and firm when that step would reach 25 ms. So every set passes the vibrate driver's `validLevels` and none exceeds 25 ms; with `minMs` = 20 ms the levels are 20/22/25 ms (earlier builds gave 20/25/30). Whether 2–3 ms apart still feels distinct is `[verify on phones]` (R12 #7); the alternative is to amend R8.3. (2) **Level set:** R8.7 allows only 3 or 4 levels, so soft, medium and firm are always kept and only the whisper can be dropped. Skipping step 2 after step 1 made soft shorter than the stored one drops a stored whisper (it was judged weaker than the longer soft tick only), and the done screen says so. The whisper `[4,4,4,4,4]` has 12 ms of on-time, more than a 5 or 10 ms soft tick, so which one feels weaker is the user's call: step 2 asks "was the first buzz **weaker** than the second?" and keeps the whisper only when it was felt and weaker; the same or stronger drops it, so the levels stay weakest first. A phone that cannot tell medium from firm still gets 3 bins; the strength multiplier is the only fallback. (3) **"I felt none"** in step 1 is not a measurement: it keeps the stored `minMs` (or the 8 ms default), is never saved over a stored value, shows as "(kept: no pulse felt)" or "(default: no pulse felt)", and suggests checking vibration settings and battery saver (R12 #11). (4) **Where it runs:** R8.7 "phones that vibrate" = `navigator.vibrate` **and** Android with a touch screen (`calibrationAvailable`, the platform.ts rule of Q-73), so desktop Chrome and Chromebooks are not offered it. UML 13 used to run the audio steps on iPhone, but with one output there is nothing to align. (5) **Skip and Cancel:** a skipped step keeps what the device already has saved (or the R8.3 default); with nothing re-measured, nothing is written. Cancel, offered on every step, saves nothing and stops the wizard's own output (a raw pattern, a scheduled sound beat). (6) The probes, the levels and the beat each play as one raw `navigator.vibrate` pattern, bypassing the driver's levels and delay that are being calibrated. They therefore bypass the engine too: no honesty gate, no rate cap, and no `spherex:haptic` debug event, so the D-20 pulse indicator does not flash for them. They respect the vibration on/off setting: with vibration off the wizard plays nothing and says so (as the sound beat respects sound on/off). The 700 ms gap and the 5 ms step are `[verify on phones]` in `tuning.ts`. (7) **Answers wait for the playback:** the step 1 and step 2 answer buttons are disabled until the pulses or buzzes have been played once (the group label says "play them first"). (8) **Feedback (R9, WCAG 3.3.1):** status and failure messages ("Not enough taps", "too uneven", "Calibration cancelled") are visible in a `role="status"` line under the step text; step titles are not repeated there, because focus moves to each step's heading (on open too, scrolled into view). During a beat the tap area names its channel ("Vibration beat: tap with each buzz" / "Sound beat: tap with each beep") and a counter shows "Beat n of 10". Closing returns focus to the opener (`returnFocusRef`). Step 3 asks to turn the phone's media volume up and silent mode off, which the page cannot check. (9) **Restart and save failures:** Cancel without a host handler returns to the intro and forgets the cancelled run (answers, played state, offsets), so a later run starts from this device's values. Playing the pulses or buzzes announces the next question, and a visible line says the answer buttons unlock after playing. A failed save is said in the status line. **Status note (9 Nov 2026):** deferred by Q-132 (phones after the event); at the event the unlock button reads "Turn on sound". | Atish | Superseded by D-39 |
| Q-75 | Tap-along method for `avOffsetMs` (issue #10; R8.7 step 3 "method details [verify]", R8.9, R11). Implemented in `frontend/src/haptics/calibration.ts` (`tapLag`, `computeAvOffset`): 10 beats at 800 ms per channel; vibration only (firm 25 ms ticks, one raw pattern), then sound only (the `measured` clean tone, scheduled by the audio driver with its own lookahead, on a fresh clock anchor, with the offset temporarily 0). The sound beat needs sound on and a volume above 0, and says so otherwise. Each beat's reference time is when it is requested, as for a real event, so the lag includes the audio lookahead and `outputLatency` (Bluetooth shows up as a negative offset, which delays the vibration). Taps (the `pointerdown` time stamp, or Space/Enter) are matched to the nearest beat within ±400 ms, half the period, so a 150–250 ms Bluetooth lag plus reaction time does not wrap onto the next beat; the first 2 beats are warm-up; a second tap on a beat is ignored. The lag is the **median** of at least 4 matched taps, so a stray tap does not move it; a run whose median absolute deviation exceeds 60 ms is refused as too uneven (this also catches a true lag near half a period, which splits between two beats). `avOffsetMs` = round(vibration lag − sound lag); positive delays the audio, negative the vibration (R8.9). A result beyond ±400 ms is refused, and a stored one counts as corrupt. All of these are `[verify on phones]`; the R11 "offset under a threshold" value is still open. **Status note (9 Nov 2026):** deferred by Q-132 (phones after the event); at the event the unlock button reads "Turn on sound". | Atish | Superseded by D-39 |
| Q-76 | Spectrum haptics choices (issue #10; R8.3, R8.6, R9). (1) **One step per move:** when one pointer move crosses several 8 px steps, only the last plays: the earlier ones are where the finger no longer is, and the engine's 25 ms rule would drop them. (2) **Jitter:** after a noisy point (σ/flux > 0.3 at the nearest point) the gap to the next step is 8 px × (1 ± 0.3·u), with u from a 32-bit hash of (seed, step index since the gesture began) rather than a stream RNG, so a step's jitter does not depend on earlier moves. The seed defaults to 0 (`SPECTRUM_DEFAULT_SEED`); M5 should pass the card's `seed`. (3) **Outside the data:** a step beyond the spectrum's λ range plays nothing, like a quiet step. (4) **Pan** spans the pad's width (−1 left, 1 right). (5) **Keyboard:** the pad is a `role="slider"` over λ. The first arrow press starts at the end it moves away from; each press is one step and emits the same event; `aria-valuetext` gives λ, the flux as a % of the peak, and "quiet" or "noisy" (the R9 text equivalent; the chart's data table stays the full one). (6) **Pad and chart:** for now the pad draws its own normalised-flux line; M5 overlays it on `SpectrumChart` and passes the chart's λ domain (`lambdaDomain`). (7) **Pointers and keys:** one pointer drives a drag (the first finger, or the main mouse button); a second finger neither moves nor ends it. Arrow keys step once; Page Up and Page Down 10 steps; Home and End jump to the ends; a step past an edge lands on the edge. (8) A spectrum with usable flux at only one wavelength (or a zero-width range) is refused with a sentence rather than spread over the pad. (9) **Readout and labels:** the readout and slider value text follow the finger during a drag (at most once per animation frame), say "uncertain" for σ/flux > 0.3, and add "(hidden by …)" when the engine refused a tick because the honesty state hides its tag (a quiet dip is just "quiet"). The pad shows its D-02 badge as a data haptic ("Data · Measured", from `badgeForTag`; "Data · Fixture" for synthetic data, as `DataBadge`), as R8.2 asks, and its help text adds TalkBack use. The host page owns the one unlock button; the pad has none. The emit interface is named `SpectrumSink`. (10) **ARIA and badges:** the visible readout follows the finger every frame, but the slider's `aria-valuetext` / `aria-valuenow` change only at the end of a drag and on keys, so a screen reader is not flooded; before any step the value text says "Start of the spectrum, … µm; no step yet". The badge is app/Badge's `ProvenanceBadge` after "Data ·", with a visible "(synthetic)" for fixture data and "(artist's choice)" for Default. Every tick carries `channel: 'data'` (Q-85), so the pulse indicator labels it as data. **Status note (9 Nov 2026):** the phone parts apply after the event (Q-132). | Atish, Moon | Open (implemented as described); amended by D-39 (the pad is cut; the spectrum mapping stays) |
| Q-77 | R11 "dragging across a known absorption dip gives at least 3 consecutive quiet steps" at phone width (issue #10; R8.6, R11). At a 360 px viewport the pad is about 296 px wide. Over the full SPHEREx band (0.75–5 µm) that is 69.6 px/µm, so 3 quiet 8 px steps need a quiet stretch of 16–24 px, i.e. 0.23–0.35 µm of λ below 20% of the peak. A narrow feature does not reach that: a 90%-deep Gaussian dip with σ 0.2 µm (FWHM 0.47 µm) is quiet only where 1 − 0.9·g < 0.2, i.e. within 0.2·√(2 ln 1.125) ≈ 0.097 µm of its centre, a 0.194 µm stretch ≈ 13.5 px = 1–2 steps. Real SPHEREx features at R ≈ 35–130 are about as narrow as a resolution element (≈ 0.03–0.12 µm). Broad bands do pass: a T/Y-dwarf CH₄ trough over 3.0–3.8 µm (WISE 0855) gives about 7 quiet steps. Both cases are tested in `sources/spectrum.test.ts`; a 296 px test documents the narrow-dip limit. Options for Atish: (a) **zoom**: the pad takes a λ range (`lambdaDomain`), so M5 can offer zoom or follow the chart's zoom; the narrow dip passes zoomed to 3.5–5 µm, and the test page has that zoom; (b) **spacing in λ** instead of px (e.g. one step per resolution element), which keeps detail at any width but changes R8.6's "every 8 px"; (c) smaller spacing on narrow screens (more events, still under the 40/s cap); (d) run the R11 check in landscape or on pads of at least 800 px; (e) define R11's "known dip" as a broad band. Implemented: (a) only; R8.6 is unchanged. **Status note (9 Nov 2026):** the phone-width test applies after the event (Q-132). | Atish | Superseded by D-39 |
| Q-83 | "Holding still" and the source triggers (issue #11; R8.5). (1) `pointermove` does not fire while a finger is still, and sources own no timers, so `TouchHaptics.poll(now)` must be called from the viewer's frame loop while one pointer is down; after `HOLD_STILL_MS` within `HOLD_STILL_TOLERANCE_PX` the data pulse starts and replaces any texture train (one train at a time, Q-71); moving again stops it and the texture resumes. M5 wires the poll. (2) Only `teq_pulse` and `flux_pulse` wait for holding still, because R8.5 says so for them; the comet field, the nebula/nova density field and the binary pulse play while the finger moves over them; the Mira heartbeat and the T Tauri pulse follow the light-curve scrubber, not the finger. (3) Comet defaults: with no gas selected on the spectrum, the field uses the first of CO₂, H₂O, CO that has a Q in the epoch; with no epoch given, the first epoch with any Q. r is the projected distance from the nucleus in coma radii; outside the coma there is no pulse; a selected gas without a Q in the epoch refuses the field. The felt nucleus is the drawn one: the caller passes the comet plan's bodies and the tick uses the `nucleus` body's radius (without them the tick is refused as `nucleus_tick`); on the nucleus there is one firm tick and no coma pulse.
