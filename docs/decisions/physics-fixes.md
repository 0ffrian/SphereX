## Q-355 to Q-362 Phase 3: physics (issue #93)

Status for all: **Decided (Atish, 11 Nov 2026)** under D-38, except Q-361 (**Open**). In the world engine each fix is a `@deviation (#93 3.x)` block carried by `frontend/scripts/world_engine_deviations.py` (Q-354), and `frontend/src/physics/` follows the same rule. Item 3.7 was not merged into `fix/93-engines`.

- **Q-355 Critical points and a supercritical state (#93 3.1; amends Q-330 (2)).** Every fluid has its critical point (handbook constants of the pure fluid, as the NIST Chemistry WebBook lists them): water 647.10 K, 22.064 MPa (IAPWS-95); NH₃ 405.4 K, 11.33 MPa; N₂ 126.19 K, 3.3958 MPa; CH₄ 190.56 K, 4.599 MPa; CO₂ 304.13 K, 7.3773 MPa. The boiling point is capped at Tc, and at or above Pc there is no boiling. Above both Tc and Pc the state is `supercritical`, with the caption "… is past its critical point here: too hot and too dense to keep a sea, it is one supercritical fluid from the ground up" (the runaway caption keeps priority; supercritical water still counts as boiled off). The NH₃–H₂O mixture uses pure NH₃'s critical point (its critical locus depends on the mix, which the prototype does not set) **[verify]** (R12 #53). Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-356 Saturation pressure with the right latent heat (#93 3.2).** Water: Murphy and Koop 2005 (QJRMS 131, 1539), eq. 7 over ice below the triple point and eq. 10 over liquid above it; above 373.15 K Clausius–Clapeyron with the prototype's heat, from the fit's value there. 1,706 Pa at 288 K (the prototype's single heat gave 1,537 Pa). CO₂ (any fluid with `Lv`): the vaporisation heat `Lv` above the triple point and the sublimation heat below; 1.61 MPa at 250 K against 1.785 MPa (NIST; the old sublimation heat gave 3.36 MPa). The `Lv` value stays **[verify]** (R12 #46). Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-357 Tides read the preset's own orbit first (#93 3.3).** The tidal heating and the orbit extras read `p.ob` (written by `cardToWorld`: the host mass from `host_mass_msun`, the eccentricity from `orbit_ecc`, Q-363, and the obliquity) before the prototype's `ORB` table, which knows only its own presets. The host mass already reached the engine; the eccentricity now comes from the card. Owner: Atish.
- **Q-358 Turnover time from Reiners et al. 2022 (#93 3.4; amends Q-231, Q-310, Q-329 (1)).** The field law (199 G Ro^−1.26, 2050 G Ro^−0.11) is Reiners et al. 2022 (A&A 662, A41), whose Rossby number uses τ = 12.3 d (L/L☉)^−1/2; the Wright et al. 2011 mass fit belonged to a different Rossby scale. Now τ = 12.3 d (L/L☉)^−1/2 with L = R² (Teff / 5772 K)⁴ from the radius and temperature the engine uses; the card's `lum_lsun` (Gaia FLAME, Q-363) is not an input (corrected by Q-374: this entry first said the card's luminosity was used when present). The solar anchor uses the same τ: Ro☉ = 25.4 / 12.3 = 2.07 and 80 G (was 1.76 and 98 G); spot coverage is relative to it, so the Sun's is unchanged. Barnard's Star from its card (0.2022 R☉ Gaia FLAME, 3,243 K): τ about 193 d (was 101 d; Q-374 replaces the 208 d first given here). `model_dependent`. Same law in `physics/stellar.ts` and the star engine (deviation 6). The 12.3 d coefficient is **[verify]** (R12 #52). Spec: [R7.5](../rendering/renderers/stars.md#r75-stars-star-star_mira-star_ttauri). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-359 A catalogue Teq is `derived`; no albedo from it (#93 3.5; amends D-12, D-21's haptics bullet, Q-330 (7)).** An equilibrium temperature is computed with an assumed albedo and heat redistribution, never measured, so the archive's `pl_eqt` is `derived` with a formula and an assumption (it was `measured`); an archive-calculated one says zero Bond albedo and full redistribution. `cardToWorld` no longer inverts `teq_K` into a Bond albedo (TOI-700 d and 55 Cnc e were about 25 K too hot with A = 0); a measured surface temperature is still inverted (Titan). With neither and no albedo on the card the prototype's 0.3 is used and listed as an assumption. The confidence score counts a derived value with an assumption at 0.5 (`DERIVED_ASSUMED_C`), so exoplanet scores drop about 7.5 points (Q-362). Haptics: in "Measured only" the `teq_pulse` is silent unless `teq_K` is `measured` (no archive Teq is now). Spec: [R3.2](../rendering/data-contract.md#r32-objectcard-produced-by-the-backend-get-v1objectsidcard), [R8.5](../rendering/haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile), [A3.8](../architecture/object-sources.md). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-360 Runaway greenhouse with a mass-dependent inner edge (#93 3.6).** The inner edge is Kopparapu et al. 2014 (ApJL 787, L29, Table 1; checked against arXiv:1404.5292) for 0.1, 1 and 5 Earth masses, interpolated in log mass, the nearest row outside that range; the limit is `model_dependent`. During a runaway the outgoing radiation is held at the steam limit, 282 W m⁻² (Goldblatt et al. 2013, Nat. Geosci. 6, 661), until the surface reaches the post-runaway temperature: about 1,500 K under 50 bar of steam and 1,800 K under 260 bar (Boukrouche et al. 2021, ApJ 919, 130, as quoted by Dorn and Lichtenberg 2021, ApJL 922, L4), interpolated in ln P between the two points, with the steam pressure taken to be the surface pressure, **[verify]** (R12 #51). Same rule in `physics/climateBudget.ts` (`RUNAWAY_OLR_LIMIT`, `POST_RUNAWAY`). **Amended by:** Q-373 (12 Nov 2026): the post-runaway temperature is no longer extrapolated (null below 50 bar, held at 1,800 K above 260 bar), so K2-18 b keeps about 401 K with no lava; this entry's first look (about 1,167 K, lava under steam haze) and its "Moon to confirm the look" are moot. Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-361 Climate heat transport and sea ice (#93 3.7, not merged).** Implemented on branch `fix/93-physics` (commit c121c94) and not merged: Earth's equator-to-pole contrast reached 28.2 K (target 40 to 60 K), sea ice 0.1 % (target 5 to 10 %) and the energy residual 35.6 W m⁻² (target under 1 W m⁻²), and TRAPPIST-1 f became a snowball. Causes found in the prototype: snow melt costs heat but falling snow releases none (about 31 W m⁻² leak on Earth); the relative-humidity emissivity term double-counts the vapour greenhouse; the polar ocean stays ice-free; `physics/climateBudget.ts` has drifted from the engine (freezing point, cloud constants, snow-melt rate). Owner: Atish, with Moon's sign-off. **Status:** Open.
- **Q-362 Confidence-score corrections (#93 3.8; amends Q-221, Q-222, Q-327).** (1) An input drawn through a conversion (a giant's colour from Teq, a star's field from its rotation) scores `min(c of the value, c of the conversion's tag)` with the weaker tag. (2) Star tables drop proper motion (it does not change the picture): its weight 5 goes to the rotation period (`star`, `star_ttauri`; through the `model_dependent` field law; without a period the Sun's Rossby number, `inferred_from_analog`) and, for Miras, to the pulsation period (weight 20). (3) A giant's colour from Teq is `model_dependent`. Pinned scores in `backend/tests/test_confidence.py` after the integration (old → new): Barnard's Star 0.6 → 0.575, ε Eri 0.6 → 0.575 (measured rotation now scored), RW Aur 0.5 → 0.45, Procyon 0.6951 → 0.6451, the Sun 0.65 → 0.675 (now above Procyon), Jupiter and Saturn 0.55 → 0.5, TRAPPIST-1 e 0.5788 → 0.5038, TRAPPIST-1 f 0.5873 → 0.5123, Kepler-186 f 0.4831 → 0.4081, TOI-700 d 0.5358 → 0.4608, 55 Cnc e 0.5854 → 0.5104, K2-18 b 0.5191 → 0.4441, HD 189733 b 0.5391 → 0.4141, WASP-39 b 0.5275 → 0.4025 (these include Q-359). Spec: [A4.7](../architecture/pipeline-card.md#a47-stage-7-renderparams). Owner: Atish.

## Q-373 to Q-380 Physics and data follow-up (issue #93)

From the red/blue review of the #93 physics and data (items A1 to A11, commits 6f03808 to 2a0382c on `fix/93-engines`, 12 Nov 2026). Q-373 is **Decided (Atish, 12 Nov 2026)**; the others are **Open (built)**, each with the person to confirm it. Each amends the entries it names, and those entries carry a back-link.

- **Q-373 The post-runaway temperature only within its data; K2-18 b keeps the prototype's look (#93 A1; amends Q-360).** The post-runaway surface temperature `Tpost` (Boukrouche et al. 2021 via Dorn and Lichtenberg 2021, Q-360) is used only between its two points, 50 and 260 bar of steam, interpolated in ln P. Above 260 bar it is held at 1,800 K. Below 50 bar the models give no value, so `Tpost` is null: the runaway verdict and its haze stand, the grey-greenhouse surface temperature is kept, there is no lava, and `ebStep` skips the steam-limit cap. The extrapolation below 50 bar is removed. Same rule in `physics/climateBudget.ts` (`postRunawayTs` returns null below 50 bar). K2-18 b at the artifact's 8 bar is about 401 K, a runaway steam world with no lava: the approved D-37 prototype look, chosen by Atish on 12 Nov 2026, so Q-360's lava look and "Moon to confirm the look" are moot. **Caveats:** K2-18 b's mass and radius allow a thick hydrogen envelope (a sub-Neptune), so a rocky surface under 8 bar is the artifact's setting, not a finding; `cardToWorld` adds "K2-18 b's mass and radius allow a thick hydrogen envelope; the 8 bar surface is the artifact's setting" to the pressure note. The runaway verdict is marginal: it lies within the error of `pl_insol`, and the Kopparapu et al. 2014 inner edge is taken at its 5 M⊕ row (the mass is clamped there). The two post-runaway points stay **[verify]** (R12 #51). Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish. **Status:** Decided (Atish, 12 Nov 2026).
- **Q-374 The turnover time reads the card's radius and temperature, not its luminosity (#93 A9; corrects Q-358 and Q-363).** τ = 12.3 d (L/L☉)^−1/2 takes L = R² (Teff / 5772 K)⁴ from the radius and temperature the star engine draws (`protoStar1.js` `tauC`, `physics/stellar.ts` `turnoverTimeDays`). The card's `lum_lsun` (Gaia FLAME, Q-363) is shown on the card but is not an engine input; `cardToStar` no longer computes the unused `luminosity` field. Barnard's Star from its card (R 0.2022 R☉ Gaia FLAME, T 3,243 K, so L about 0.0041 L☉): τ about 193 d. This replaces every 208 d or 215 d figure. The 12.3 d coefficient stays **[verify]** (R12 #52). Spec: [R7.5](../rendering/renderers/stars.md#r75-stars-star-star_mira-star_ttauri). Owner: Atish. **Status:** Open (built); Atish to confirm.
- **Q-375 The starlight takes the card's insolation first (#93 A5; amends Q-363).** On the generic `cardToWorld` path the world engine's starlight comes from `insolation_searth` (`pl_insol`), L = S a², R_star = √L (5772 K / T_star)². One reference computed it from its own L and a, so it is self-consistent, while `host_lum_lsun` and `orbit_au` can come from different papers (K2-18 b: L / a² = 1.24 S⊕ against `pl_insol` 1.005). `host_lum_lsun` / a² is used only when the card has no insolation. When both exist and differ by more than 5 %, a line goes in `mismatches` and the insolation is used. Planets drawn from an artifact preset are unaffected. Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish. **Status:** Open (built); Atish to confirm.
- **Q-376 Eccentricity limits (#93 A6; amends Q-363 and Q-366).** The archive column set is version 4 (`COLUMNS_VERSION`), adding `pl_orbeccenlim` (1 an upper limit, −1 a lower limit, 0 a value). A nonzero flag makes `orbit_ecc` `model_dependent`, with no `err`, the flag in `source` and the assumption "archive limit flag: an upper or lower limit, not a measured value". Kepler-186 f's e = 0.04 is now `model_dependent`. On the generic `cardToWorld` path a `model_dependent` eccentricity (a limit, or e fixed at 0 in the fit) is listed under "Filled in, not measured". Spec: [A3.8](../architecture/object-sources.md#a38-nasa-exoplanet-archive), [R3.2](../rendering/data-contract.md#r32-objectcard-produced-by-the-backend-get-v1objectsidcard). Owner: Atish. **Status:** Open (built); Atish to confirm.
- **Q-377 Phase-curve rows: one refusal, one replacement (#93 A2, A3; amends Q-364).** In `backend/assets/literature.toml`: (1) **55 Cnc e**'s `hotspot_offset_deg` is a refusal row, because two analyses of the same Spitzer data disagree: Demory et al. 2016 (41 ± 12° east) and Mercier et al. 2022 (AJ 164, 204: −12 +21/−18°). The day and night rows keep their values; their notes now cite Mercier et al. 2022 (dayside 3,771 +669/−520 K, nightside below 1,649 K at 2σ) and the JWST dayside changing from visit to visit (Hu et al. 2024, Nature 630, 609; Patel et al. 2024, A&A 690), so no single dayside temperature holds. The 2,700 K row is the hot-spot hemisphere and is shown as "Hot-spot hemisphere (brightness temperature)" (Q-379). (2) **HD 189733 b**'s offset is 20.1 ± 5.5° east, `derived`, from Knutson et al. 2012 (ApJ 754, 22): the flux maximum 2.98 ± 0.82 h before the centre of the secondary eclipse at 4.5 µm, × 360 / (P × 24) with P = 2.21857 d (the formula is in the row). It replaces Bell et al. 2021's 33.7 (+2.5/−2.2)° (MNRAS 504, 3316), whose own Sect. 6.2 finds that for this planet the fitted error underpredicts the scatter between detector models by about 21 times (about 51°). Spec: [A3.13](../architecture/object-sources.md#a313-literature-registry-literaturetoml). Owner: Kevin. **Status:** Open (built); Kevin to confirm the readings.
- **Q-378 Gaia FLAME luminosity past three decimals; new cache keys and `ASSET_VERSION` 13 (#93 A4; amends Q-366).** The IRSA CSV writes `lum_flame` with three decimals, so Barnard's Star came back as 0.003 L☉ with its 16th and 84th percentiles rounded to the same number (no `err`), and a star below 0.0005 L☉ as 0. The query now selects `lum_flame`, `lum_flame_lower` and `lum_flame_upper` × 1e6, and the parser shifts the decimal point back in the text (not a float division); a scaled 0 is unknown, not zero. Barnard's Star's `lum_lsun` is 0.003398 ± 3.4e-7 L☉. The Gaia cache key is `cols="msc1-flame3"` and the archive's column set is version 4 (Q-376), so every Gaia star and archive planet re-keys. `ASSET_VERSION` is 13 (Q-376 to Q-378); the demo pack and reference assets are re-baked. The A15 re-warm is Q-366's, with the new keys. Spec: [A3.2](../architecture/data-sources.md#a32-gaia-dr3), [A4.4](../architecture/pipeline-card.md#a44-stage-4-check-for-a-saved-asset), [A10.2](../architecture/frontend-platform.md#a102-running-the-event-build). Owner: Atish. **Status:** Open (built); Atish to confirm.

- **Q-380 The gas caption's rate at the measured frame rate (#93 A7; amends Q-343).** When a giant needs more than 3 solver steps a frame, the caption's "One second here is about N hours" is computed at the frame rate the machine really draws (`W.dt93`, an exponential average of the frame time with weight 0.05; 60 fps before the first gas frame), not at an assumed 60 fps. Hours are rounded to 1 h below 10 h and to 2 h above, and the shown value changes only when the new one is 15 % away, so the caption does not flicker; it adds "so this follows the frame rate". The same deviation is in `world_engine_deviations.py` and `extract-world-engine.py`. Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish. **Status:** Open (built); Atish to confirm.
