## Q-355 to Q-362 Phase 3: physics (issue #93)

Status for all: **Decided (Atish, 11 Nov 2026)** under D-38, except Q-361 (**Open**). In the world engine each fix is a `@deviation (#93 3.x)` block carried by `frontend/scripts/world_engine_deviations.py` (Q-354), and `frontend/src/physics/` follows the same rule. Item 3.7 was not merged into `fix/93-engines`.

- **Q-355 Critical points and a supercritical state (#93 3.1; amends Q-330 (2)).** Every fluid has its critical point (handbook constants of the pure fluid, as the NIST Chemistry WebBook lists them): water 647.10 K, 22.064 MPa (IAPWS-95); NH₃ 405.4 K, 11.33 MPa; N₂ 126.19 K, 3.3958 MPa; CH₄ 190.56 K, 4.599 MPa; CO₂ 304.13 K, 7.3773 MPa. The boiling point is capped at Tc, and at or above Pc there is no boiling. Above both Tc and Pc the state is `supercritical`, with the caption "… is past its critical point here: too hot and too dense to keep a sea, it is one supercritical fluid from the ground up" (the runaway caption keeps priority; supercritical water still counts as boiled off). The NH₃–H₂O mixture uses pure NH₃'s critical point (its critical locus depends on the mix, which the prototype does not set) **[verify]** (R12 #53). Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-356 Saturation pressure with the right latent heat (#93 3.2).** Water: Murphy and Koop 2005 (QJRMS 131, 1539), eq. 7 over ice below the triple point and eq. 10 over liquid above it; above 373.15 K Clausius–Clapeyron with the prototype's heat, from the fit's value there. 1,706 Pa at 288 K (the prototype's single heat gave 1,537 Pa). CO₂ (any fluid with `Lv`): the vaporisation heat `Lv` above the triple point and the sublimation heat below; 1.61 MPa at 250 K against 1.785 MPa (NIST; the old sublimation heat gave 3.36 MPa). The `Lv` value stays **[verify]** (R12 #46). Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-357 Tides read the preset's own orbit first (#93 3.3).** The tidal heating and the orbit extras read `p.ob` (written by `cardToWorld`: the host mass from `host_mass_msun`, the eccentricity from `orbit_ecc`, Q-363, and the obliquity) before the prototype's `ORB` table, which knows only its own presets. The host mass already reached the engine; the eccentricity now comes from the card. Owner: Atish.
- **Q-358 Turnover time from Reiners et al. 2022 (#93 3.4; amends Q-231, Q-310, Q-329 (1)).** The field law (199 G Ro^−1.26, 2050 G Ro^−0.11) is Reiners et al. 2022 (A&A 662, A41), whose Rossby number uses τ = 12.3 d (L/L☉)^−1/2; the Wright et al. 2011 mass fit belonged to a different Rossby scale. Now τ = 12.3 d (L/L☉)^−1/2 with the card's luminosity (`lum_lsun`, Gaia FLAME, Q-363) when it has one, else L = R² (Teff / 5772 K)⁴. The solar anchor uses the same τ: Ro☉ = 25.4 / 12.3 = 2.07 and 80 G (was 1.76 and 98 G); spot coverage is relative to it, so the Sun's is unchanged. Barnard's Star (0.1867 R☉, 3,195 K in the test): τ about 208 d (was 101 d). `model_dependent`. Same law in `physics/stellar.ts` and the star engine (deviation 6). The 12.3 d coefficient is **[verify]** (R12 #52). Spec: [R7.5](../rendering/renderers/stars.md#r75-stars-star-star_mira-star_ttauri). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-359 A catalogue Teq is `derived`; no albedo from it (#93 3.5; amends D-12, D-21's haptics bullet, Q-330 (7)).** An equilibrium temperature is computed with an assumed albedo and heat redistribution, never measured, so the archive's `pl_eqt` is `derived` with a formula and an assumption (it was `measured`); an archive-calculated one says zero Bond albedo and full redistribution. `cardToWorld` no longer inverts `teq_K` into a Bond albedo (TOI-700 d and 55 Cnc e were about 25 K too hot with A = 0); a measured surface temperature is still inverted (Titan). With neither and no albedo on the card the prototype's 0.3 is used and listed as an assumption. The confidence score counts a derived value with an assumption at 0.5 (`DERIVED_ASSUMED_C`), so exoplanet scores drop about 7.5 points (Q-362). Haptics: in "Measured only" the `teq_pulse` is silent unless `teq_K` is `measured` (no archive Teq is now). Spec: [R3.2](../rendering/data-contract.md#r32-objectcard-produced-by-the-backend-get-v1objectsidcard), [R8.5](../rendering/haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile), [A3.8](../architecture/object-sources.md). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-360 Runaway greenhouse with a mass-dependent inner edge (#93 3.6).** The inner edge is Kopparapu et al. 2014 (ApJL 787, L29, Table 1; checked against arXiv:1404.5292) for 0.1, 1 and 5 Earth masses, interpolated in log mass, the nearest row outside that range; the limit is `model_dependent`. During a runaway the outgoing radiation is held at the steam limit, 282 W m⁻² (Goldblatt et al. 2013, Nat. Geosci. 6, 661), until the surface reaches the post-runaway temperature: about 1,500 K under 50 bar of steam and 1,800 K under 260 bar (Boukrouche et al. 2021, ApJ 919, 130, as quoted by Dorn and Lichtenberg 2021, ApJL 922, L4), interpolated in ln P and extrapolated outside, with the steam pressure taken to be the surface pressure, **[verify]** (R12 #51). K2-18 b goes from 401 K to about 1,167 K and is drawn as lava under steam haze. Same rule in `physics/climateBudget.ts` (`RUNAWAY_OLR_LIMIT`, `POST_RUNAWAY`). Moon to confirm the look. Spec: [R7.7.1](../rendering/renderers/world-engine.md#r771-world-engine-values). Owner: Atish.
- **Q-361 Climate heat transport and sea ice (#93 3.7, not merged).** Implemented on branch `fix/93-physics` (commit c121c94) and not merged: Earth's equator-to-pole contrast reached 28.2 K (target 40 to 60 K), sea ice 0.1 % (target 5 to 10 %) and the energy residual 35.6 W m⁻² (target under 1 W m⁻²), and TRAPPIST-1 f became a snowball. Causes found in the prototype: snow melt costs heat but falling snow releases none (about 31 W m⁻² leak on Earth); the relative-humidity emissivity term double-counts the vapour greenhouse; the polar ocean stays ice-free; `physics/climateBudget.ts` has drifted from the engine (freezing point, cloud constants, snow-melt rate). Owner: Atish, with Moon's sign-off. **Status:** Open.
- **Q-362 Confidence-score corrections (#93 3.8; amends Q-221, Q-222, Q-327).** (1) An input drawn through a conversion (a giant's colour from Teq, a star's field from its rotation) scores `min(c of the value, c of the conversion's tag)` with the weaker tag. (2) Star tables drop proper motion (it does not change the picture): its weight 5 goes to the rotation period (`star`, `star_ttauri`; through the `model_dependent` field law; without a period the Sun's Rossby number, `inferred_from_analog`) and, for Miras, to the pulsation period (weight 20). (3) A giant's colour from Teq is `model_dependent`. Pinned scores in `backend/tests/test_confidence.py` after the integration (old → new): Barnard's Star 0.6 → 0.575, ε Eri 0.6 → 0.575 (measured rotation now scored), RW Aur 0.5 → 0.45, Procyon 0.6951 → 0.6451, the Sun 0.65 → 0.675 (now above Procyon), Jupiter and Saturn 0.55 → 0.5, TRAPPIST-1 e 0.5788 → 0.5038, TRAPPIST-1 f 0.5873 → 0.5123, Kepler-186 f 0.4831 → 0.4081, TOI-700 d 0.5358 → 0.4608, 55 Cnc e 0.5854 → 0.5104, K2-18 b 0.5191 → 0.4441, HD 189733 b 0.5391 → 0.4141, WASP-39 b 0.5275 → 0.4025 (these include Q-359). Spec: [A4.7](../architecture/pipeline-card.md#a47-stage-7-renderparams). Owner: Atish.
