# Decisions: Shell surfaces and the Build split view

> **Part of:** the decision log ([index](README.md)) · **Holds:** the tool rail and the island, the object panel and object card, the Build split view and moulding, the full-bleed layout · **Authority:** highest; an entry here wins over [rendering/](../rendering/README.md) and [architecture/](../architecture/README.md) (see the index for precedence)

## D-38 Design language: Graphite, Inter, tight radii, Animate UI

- **Context:** the Accuracy popover and the Details pane showed too much at once, and their selected states (a fluorescent green border on a translucent fill) read as generated. Moon asked for both panels to be simplified and for the UI to follow the design language of Penrose, the team lead's other project, with Animate UI as the component library.
- **Decision (Moon, 11 Nov 2026):** the look and feel of every screen follows [docs/design-language.md](../design-language.md): near-black surfaces (`--bg #08090b`, `--s1 #0f1114`; darker than Penrose's Graphite so panels sit with the sky, Moon 11 Nov 2026), Inter only (bundled, D-24), radii on one scale (10, 8, 6, 4px, no pills outside the rail and docks), tonal selected states (`--ac` / `--on-ac`), round switches, explanations folded into rows that open, and Animate UI's components and spring tokens. The skill `sx-design-language` loads it.
- **Approved reference:** the Accuracy and Details preview, https://claude.ai/artifact/GoA2dKYrTKJKXprmhzkTy1. Accuracy keeps the provenance bar and key, the general formula C = Σ w c / Σ w with what each letter means, and folds the values of c, the worked sum and the 3D gate into rows. The big score number and its band words are removed. Details keeps Light and View (segmented controls), Field lines (a switch) and Fire a big flare; Replay, New seed, the separate Close-up view button, the arrow and zoom row and Pause are removed from the pane; the flare text and chart, What is drawn, Shown as a default and Not drawn fold into rows.
- **Precedence:** for look and feel this wins over the mock-up (`mockup/spherex-ui.html`), which still sets layout, structure and wording (D-01 stands: the mock-up is visual input, not a spec). Open points: Q-326 (light theme), Q-327 (the "artist's impression" line) Q-328 (Pause) and Q-329 (Show level in the pane).
- **Spec:** [design-language.md](../design-language.md).
