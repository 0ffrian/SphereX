# Decisions: Haptics and sound: audio

> **Part of:** the decision log ([index](README.md)) · **Holds:** audio pairing (D-10), spectrum pitch and voice hints, the body tone (Q-133, Q-168 to Q-172) and the Track path sound (Q-134, Q-174) (haptics design rules and the API are in [haptics-design.md](haptics-design.md)) · **Authority:** highest; an entry here wins over [rendering/](../rendering/README.md) and [architecture/](../architecture/README.md) (see the index for precedence)

## D-10 Audio pairing on every platform

- **Decision:**
  - The audio driver is no longer only a fallback. It plays **alongside** the vibration on every platform, with its own on/off switch and volume.
  - Every `HapticEvent` goes to both drivers.
  - Audio carries continuous strength (gain), pitch, stereo pan and provenance, which is shown by timbre: clean tone for `measured`, grainy click for `artistic`.
  - Spec: rendering doc §8.9.
- **Amended by:** Q-132 (at the event the audio driver is the only output; there is no vibration to pair with) and Q-133 (body interaction is one continuous tone, [R8.9.1](../rendering/haptics/audio-pairing.md#r891-body-tone-from-the-height-map-q-133)); sustained timbres by tag are proposed in Q-170.

| Q-70 | Spectrum pitch follows λ (issue #9). R8.6 and R8.9 give `pitch` from λ, "the same λ→pitch mapping as the Listen sweep"; the mock-up's sweep (`mockup/spherex-ui.html`) maps **brightness** to pitch (220 Hz × 2^(2.4·normalised flux)). Implemented as the specs say, in `frontend/src/haptics/pitch.ts`: pitch = λ over the SPHEREx band 0.75–5.0 µm (A3), so the same λ sounds the same on every object, and frequency = 220 Hz × 2^(2.4·pitch), the mock-up's range. Brightness is carried by gain. Open for Atish (D-13): the λ range (fixed band or each spectrum's own range), the direction (longer λ higher, as now, or lower) and the Hz range; all are `CREATIVE placeholder` constants in `tuning.ts`. **Amended by:** Q-133 and Q-134 (pitch = λ stands for the spectrum, the Listen sweep and the Track exposure ticks; body interaction uses pitch = height, R8.9.1). | Atish | Open (implemented as described) |
| Q-72 | Audio `voice` hint on `HapticEvent` (issue #9; D-12, R8.9, Q-19). D-12 gives per-kind sounds (gas giant bands: a tone whose pitch follows latitude; ocean: filtered-noise surf; land: clicks), but a `HapticEvent` only carries `tag`, and R8.9 picks the timbre from the tag. Added to the hand-written contract: optional `voice?: 'tone' \| 'click' \| 'surf' \| 'chime'`, ignored by the vibrate driver. Rule in `drivers/audio.ts`: the tag picks the family and the voice refines it. `artistic` is always noise, so it keeps its grainy character (D-12 "every texture sound uses the `artistic` timbre"): `surf` is low-passed noise, anything else the 4 ms grain, band-passed at the pitch when there is one (so a band tone is a pitched grain). The other tags are always tones: `chime` is a bell (the Listen sweep's gas lines), anything else the clean tone (`measured`) or the softer tone (Estimated); they never become noise. The pitched grain, the surf filter, the softer tone and the bell are `CREATIVE placeholder` values in `tuning.ts`. Gain is continuous in strength with an audible floor (`AUDIO_FLOOR_STRENGTH`, a placeholder), so strength 0, which still vibrates at the lowest level (the R8.5 ocean swell), is also heard: R8.9 "both channels or neither". A quiet dip stays silent because the source emits nothing. A strength that is not a finite number is refused by the engine. **Amended by:** Q-133 (body interaction is one continuous tone, R8.9.1); sustained timbres by tag are proposed in Q-170. | Atish | Open (implemented as described) |

| Q-168 | **Mesh height for the body tone on `asteroid_shape` (Q-133; the demo body 433 Eros).** Q-133 makes body sound a continuous tone that follows "the height", but a shape model has no height map. Proposed (R8.4 mesh source item 2): height = the distance from the model's centre to the `three-mesh-bvh` hit point minus the radius, in that direction, of a reference ellipsoid whose half-axes are the model's bounding-box half-extents (model frame). Pitch 0 and 1 are the lowest and highest heights over all vertices, computed once per model. The tone carries the mesh's tag (`measured`, as R8.2 tags mesh-normal ticks), so it plays in every honesty state; the reference surface only sets where the pitch scale starts. Alternatives: radius from the centre (tracks Eros's elongation, not its relief), or a best-fit triaxial ellipsoid; or tag the tone `derived` with the formula, which would silence it in Measured only. Not built: the raycast is missing (Q-78). Spec: [R8.4](../rendering/haptics/sources.md#r84-event-sources) (mesh source item 2), [R8.9.1](../rendering/haptics/audio-pairing.md#r891-body-tone-from-the-height-map-q-133), [R7.1](../rendering/renderers/asteroid.md#r71-asteroid_shape-bennu-eros-vesta-damit-objects), [R8.5](../rendering/haptics/profiles.md#r85-per-type-haptic-profile). | Atish | Proposed |
| Q-169 | **Tone stream in the haptics API (Q-133, Q-134; R8.8).** `HapticKind` and the Q-71 trains are discrete; the body tone and the Track path voice are sustained, continuously re-pitched sounds. Proposed: `startTone(id, ToneParams)`, `updateTone(id, Partial<ToneParams>)`, `stopTone(id)` on `HapticEngine`, ids `'body'` and `'path'`, `ToneParams = {pitch, strength, tag, pan?, voice?, channel?}`; a `ToneCapableDriver` (audio only; the vibrate driver ignores tones). The engine checks strength and applies the honesty filter on start and on tag changes (a hidden tag stops the tone); updates are coalesced to one per animation frame and do not count against the 40 events/s cap; fade in `TONE_FADE_IN_S` 0.03 s, glide `TONE_GLIDE_S` 0.02 s, fade out `TONE_FADE_OUT_S` 0.06 s (placeholders in `tuning.ts`); `stop()`, page hide and the sound switch stop every tone. Hand-written in `frontend/src/contract/haptics.ts` (Q-19). Spec: [R8.8](../rendering/haptics/engine-api.md#r88-engine-api) (tone stream), [R8.8.1](../rendering/haptics/wiring.md#r881-wiring-checklist-m5) item 4. | Atish | Proposed |
