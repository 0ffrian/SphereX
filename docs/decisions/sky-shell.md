> **Part of:** the decision log ([index](README.md)) · **Holds:** the app shell, the sky view, the flipbook UI, the rail and the island, the Build split view, sky endpoints and sky tiles · **Authority:** highest; an entry here wins over [rendering/](../rendering/README.md) and [architecture/](../architecture/README.md) (see the index for precedence)

## D-35 Local copy of sky survey tiles for the sky background

- **Context:** the sky-view prototype (branch `feat/sky-view-prototype`) draws IRSA's 2MASS colour HiPS as an all-sky background. `scripts.sky_mirror` copies the tiles to `{SX_CACHE_DIR}/sky/` so the demo runs offline (D-24, D-25). IRSA's `properties` files for 2MASS colour and AllWISE colour say `hips_status = public mirror unclonable`; 2MASS colour also says `hips_license = ODbL-1.0`, `hips_copyright = CNRS/Unistra` and `obs_copyright = University of Massachusetts & IPAC/Caltech`; AllWISE says `obs_copyright = IPAC/NASA`.
- **Decision (Atish, 7 Nov 2026):** keeping a local copy is acceptable for this project. Shift & Spectrum is a non-commercial hackathon entry; the copy stays in the local cache (outside git) on the dev machines and the Asus A15, and is not redistributed or offered as a public HiPS mirror.
- **Conditions:**
  - The UI always shows the survey credit next to the sky (the `credit` string in `backend/app/core/sky/surveys.py`); ODbL and the 2MASS and WISE terms ask for attribution whatever the use.
  - `scripts.sky_mirror` keeps refusing an `unclonable` survey unless `--allow-unclonable` is passed, so the choice stays explicit.
  - Mirror politely: at most 4 parallel requests, retries with backoff.
- **Budget:** under 1 GB on dev machines (orders 0–4, about 357 MB measured on 7 Nov 2026); up to 50 GB on the A15. The A15 depth is Q-88.
- **Numbering:** recorded as D-32 on the sky-view branch; renumbered D-35 when main was merged in (8 Nov 2026), because main's D-32 (tier fallback chain) was accepted first. Earlier PR #49 comments that say D-32 for the sky tiles mean this entry.
- **Spec:** [A21.7](../architecture/sky-shell-surfaces.md#a217-sky-image-credit-never-blank) (credit placement and lines).
