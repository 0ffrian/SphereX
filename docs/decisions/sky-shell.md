## D-35 Local copy of sky survey tiles for the sky background

- **Context:** the sky-view prototype (branch `feat/sky-view-prototype`) draws IRSA's 2MASS colour HiPS as an all-sky background. `scripts.sky_mirror` copies the tiles to `{SX_CACHE_DIR}/sky/` so the demo runs offline (D-24, D-25). IRSA's `properties` files for 2MASS colour and AllWISE colour say `hips_status = public mirror unclonable`; 2MASS colour also says `hips_license = ODbL-1.0`, `hips_copyright = CNRS/Unistra` and `obs_copyright = University of Massachusetts & IPAC/Caltech`; AllWISE says `obs_copyright = IPAC/NASA`.
- **Decision (Atish, 7 Nov 2026):** keeping a local copy is acceptable for this project. Shift & Spectrum is a non-commercial hackathon entry; the copy stays in the local cache (outside git) on the dev machines and the Asus A15, and is not redistributed or offered as a public HiPS mirror.
- **Conditions:**
  - The UI always shows the survey credit next to the sky (the `credit` string in `backend/app/core/sky/surveys.py`); ODbL and the 2MASS and WISE terms ask for attribution whatever the use.
  - `scripts.sky_mirror` keeps refusing an `unclonable` survey unless `--allow-unclonable` is passed, so the choice stays explicit.
  - Mirror politely: at most 4 parallel requests, retries with backoff.
- **Budget:** under 1 GB on dev machines (orders 0–4, about 357 MB measured on 7 Nov 2026); up to 50 GB on the A15. The A15 depth is Q-88.
- **Numbering:** recorded as D-32 on the sky-view branch; renumbered D-35 when main was merged in (8 Nov 2026), because main's D-32 (tier fallback chain) was accepted first. Earlier PR #49 comments that say D-32 for the sky tiles mean this entry.
- **Spec:** [A21.7](../architecture/sky-shell-surfaces.md#a217-sky-image-credit-never-blank) (credit placement and lines).

| Q-97 | Progressive frames in the UI (interim for #41). A live `/frames` request downloads at most `SX_LIVE_CUTOUTS_PER_REQUEST` cutouts one after another (about 20 s each from IRSA on 7 Nov 2026) and lists the rest as `cutouts:deferred:<n>`. The flipbook shows and plays the frames it has and asks again (one request in flight, 0.5 s apart) until none is deferred, merging by `frame_id` without moving playback, with Stop / Continue (`frontend/src/app/flipbook/progressive.ts`). A client timeout counts as still working (up to 5 in a row, backing off 2 → 30 s); server or network errors back off 2 → 30 s and give up after 5, with Try again; a reply that still lists deferred cutouts but got no further (same frames, same deferred count) backs off the same way and gives up after 5 in a row, so a backend that answers without downloading cannot keep the client asking for ever (PR #49 review). Spectrum and composite wait for the complete list; after Stop they can be made from the frames found so far. Progress: ready = frames listed; found = ready + deferred + per-frame refusals. Issue #41 (bounded parallel pool, two-stage answer) replaces the server side; the client loop can stay. Moon owns `app/` and `state/` (D-17) and must confirm. Since issue #41 (Q-111 (5)) the server side downloads in the object job, not in the request. **Spec:** [A21.9](../architecture/sky-shell-behaviour.md#a219-transport-session-search-pill), [A21.11](../architecture/sky-shell-behaviour.md#a2111-url-hash-and-history). | Moon | Open (implemented as described) |

| Q-100 | No-data marking in the flipbook. On the fixed frame grid (`frontend/src/app/flipbook/frameGrid.ts`), grid area the shown frame does not cover is hatched dark grey (#2a2a2e/#18181b, static) instead of black, so it does not read as dark sky; it is a display marker, not a data layer, and carries no tag. Gaps of at most 1 source pixel (the normal 58/59 px variation) stay unmarked. A frame more than 1 px smaller than the object's largest frame side is "at a detector edge" (e.g. 34×59 with the object at x = 4.3); a full-size frame with the object off centre "covers only part of the picture". For objects with such frames, one steady line ("k of n frames are at a detector edge; their missing part is hatched.") shows during playback, and the per-frame caption shows only when paused or stepping, so nothing flashes (R9; `noData.ts`). The SPHEREx composite is not marked; its no-data pixels are black in the backend PNG. | Moon, Atish | Open (implemented as described) |
