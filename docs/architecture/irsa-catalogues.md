### A3.3.1 IRSA tables and columns: CatWISE2020, AllWISE and 2MASS

**IRSA table and column facts for CatWISE2020, AllWISE and 2MASS (checked 25–26 Nov 2026, #192; resolves V6 of [A22.14](discovery-hunts.md#a2214-open-items-and-items-to-verify)).** Every name below was read from IRSA's `TAP_SCHEMA.tables` and `TAP_SCHEMA.columns` (sync, `LANG=ADQL`, `FORMAT=csv`, endpoint `https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/TAP/sync`) and every column named in the table below was then returned by one real `SELECT` on rows of its table, run on 26 Nov 2026 (field RA 150.0, Dec +2.2, 0.02° cone; for 2MASS the field RA 83.82, Dec −5.39, Orion, where CatWISE and AllWISE returned no rows). Units are the `unit` column of `TAP_SCHEMA.columns`; "(none)" means that column is empty there. This records names only: it chooses no cut for any hunt and changes no adapter. Used by the H2 and H9 adapters (CatWISE masks for H4) ([A22.6](discovery-hunts.md#a226-data-sources)). [Q-51](../decisions/data-sources.md) says AllWISE is not used: that covers the overlays of [A3.3](data-sources.md#a33-wise--neowise) and [A4.9](overlays.md#a49-overlays-flag-mask-and-combine); AllWISE is recorded here for the A22 hunts only.

- **H9 flag names.** The AllWISE columns `w4snr`, `w4rchi2`, `w4nm`, `na`, `nb` and `cc_flags` that [A22.9.9](discovery-hunts.md#a2299-h9-infrared-excess-spherex-spectra-of-candidates-characterisation) cites all exist under those names in `allwise_p3as_psd`, and `n_2mass` and `r_2mass` give the 2MASS match count and offset. Whether a hunt's cut works with them is that hunt's ticket (out of scope here).
- **Traps for the adapters.** `pmra` is arcsec/yr in CatWISE2020 and mas/yr in AllWISE. `ra_pm` is at MJD 57170 in CatWISE2020 and at MJD 55400.0 in AllWISE. 2MASS `jdate` is a Julian date, the WISE epochs are MJD. IRSA writes the unit of the raw flux columns of CatWISE2020 (`w1flux` and others) as the text `'dn'`, with the quotes.
- **Still open.** The column description of CatWISE2020 `pmra` is "Apparent motion in RA" and does not say whether it includes cos δ, so the **[verify]** in [A3.3](data-sources.md#a33-wise--neowise) stays.
- **Service limits seen (R-42, [A22.11.4](discovery-hunts.md#a22114-living-with-a-gaming-machine)).** IRSA's `/TAP/capabilities` (25 Nov 2026) publishes no output-row limit. It says synchronous queries have their own 5-minute timeout "not expressible here" and gives asynchronous jobs a default and hard execution duration of 259200 s (3 days); `/TAP/async` answers (HTTP 303). The row cap of a sync query is therefore unknown and a cone sets its own `TOP`.
- **ADQL run.** Schema queries on 25 Nov 2026, row queries on 26 Nov 2026 (47 columns of `catwise_2020`, 69 of `allwise_p3as_psd` and 30 of `fp_psc`, each reply header equal to the list asked for). Tables: `SELECT table_name, description FROM TAP_SCHEMA.tables WHERE table_name LIKE '%catwise%' OR table_name LIKE '%allwise%' OR table_name LIKE '%psc%'`. Columns, once per table: `SELECT column_name, unit, datatype, ucd, description FROM TAP_SCHEMA.columns WHERE table_name = '<table>'`. Rows: `SELECT TOP 3 <every column of that table named above, comma-separated> FROM <table> WHERE CONTAINS(POINT('ICRS',ra,dec),CIRCLE('ICRS',150.0,2.2,0.02))=1` for `catwise_2020` and `allwise_p3as_psd`, and the same with the circle at (83.82, −5.39) for `fp_psc`.
