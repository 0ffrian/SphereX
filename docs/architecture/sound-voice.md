## A8 Sound and voice

At the event every sound plays in desktop Chrome on Atish's Asus TUF A15; there is no vibration (Q-132). The rendering doc owns the rules; this table gives each part's status.

| Part | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Data sweep (Listen) | Spectrum to pitch with a triangle oscillator. Bell chimes at gas wavelengths. Stereo sweep. Web Audio in the browser. The same events drive the spectrum haptics, so sound and touch match (rendering doc §8.6). | Built in the app (issue #11, `frontend/src/haptics/sound/listenSweep.ts`): pitch from λ and gain from flux through the shared `spectrumMapping`, quiet dips silent, pan left to right, chimes at the A4.3 lines (H₂O 2.7, CO₂ 4.25, CO 4.67 µm) only for gases with Q in the selected epoch, panned where the sweep is. The honesty filter applies (D-21): the tone carries the spectrum's tag and each chime its Q's tag, so a `model_dependent` Q does not chime in Measured only, and `setHonesty` stops a sound the toggle hides mid-sweep. The sweep ends with its last sound (a chime may ring past the tone). Steps are capped at 4096. A one-wavelength spectrum is not played. It uses the shared AudioContext and master bus with its limiter (`audioGraph.ts`), follows the settings volume live and honours the sound switch (Q-84). Tested on a fake AudioContext; not yet heard on the A15. The mock-up's Listen tab mapped brightness to pitch (Q-70). Not wired into the app yet (rendering doc R8.8.1). |
| Body tone | While the user feels a body, one continuous tone follows the height under the pointer: low in grooves, higher on peaks (Q-133). Timbre by the height's tag; heard from + Artistic on procedural and shader surfaces, in every state on a real mesh such as 433 Eros. Rule: rendering doc R8.9.1. | Not built. The sources emit clicks today; the tone stream (rendering doc R8.8) and mesh height are proposed. |
| Track path sound | "Hear the Shift" in the Track tool: one tick per exposure (tagged like the exposure, pitch from λ, pan from the drawn x), a soft path voice in the Estimated timbre (Horizons path, `model_dependent`, silent in Measured only) and a quiet reference click with a visible mute (Q-134). Rule: rendering doc R8.9.2; the tool itself is A19 ([track.md](moving-objects/track.md)) and A20 ([track-ui.md](moving-objects/track-ui.md)). | Not built. |
| Imagined drive | Procedural noise, gravel and thuds shaped by gravity, roughness and surface type from the card. Always labelled "Imagined". | In the mock-up only, unheard. Not built in the app: deferred by issue #11 (lowest priority). When built it stays outside the haptic driver (rendering doc §8.9). |
| Audio pairing | Every haptic event also plays as sound, with its own on/off switch and volume. At the event the audio driver is the only output. Audio carries continuous strength (gain), pitch, stereo pan and provenance by timbre: clean tone for `measured`, softer tone for Estimated, grainy noise for `artistic` (D-10, rendering doc §8.9). | Driver built (issue #9, `drivers/audio.ts`); not yet heard on the A15. |
| AI sound clips | Post-event backlog (B-03). | Not built for the event. |
| Voice control | Gemini Live. Post-event backlog (B-02). | Not built for the event. |
