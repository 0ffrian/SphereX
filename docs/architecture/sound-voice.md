## A8 Sound and voice

At the event every sound plays in desktop Chrome on Atish's Asus TUF A15; there is no vibration (Q-132). The rendering doc owns the rules; this table gives each part's status.

| Part | Detail | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Data sweep (Listen) | Spectrum to pitch with a triangle oscillator. Bell chimes at gas wavelengths. Stereo sweep. Web Audio in the browser. Long wavelength = low pitch (Q-430). | Built against fixtures (issue #11; now `frontend/src/sound/listenSweep.ts`, D-39): pitch from λ (long λ low, Q-430) and gain from flux through the shared `spectrumMapping`, quiet dips silent, pan left to right, chimes at the A4.3 lines (H₂O 2.7, CO₂ 4.25, CO 4.67 µm) only for gases with Q in the selected epoch, panned where the sweep is. The honesty filter applies (D-21): the tone carries the spectrum's tag and each chime its Q's tag, so a `model_dependent` Q does not chime in Measured only, and `setHonesty` stops a sound the toggle hides mid-sweep. The sweep ends with its last sound (a chime may ring past the tone). Steps are capped at 4096. A one-wavelength spectrum is not played. It uses the shared AudioContext and plays into the spectrum bus, ducking the ambience while it plays (R13.9), then the master and its limiter (`audioGraph.ts`); follows the settings volume live and honours the sound switch (Q-84). Tested on a fake AudioContext; not yet heard on the A15. The mock-up's Listen tab mapped brightness to pitch (Q-70). Wired into the sky tool's Spectrum panel as its Sweep (#115, Q-446). The Hear it panel keeps its own sweep (`app/build/hearAudio.ts`), now on the same `pitch.ts` law. |
| Body tone | Deferred (D-39). While the user feels a body, one continuous tone follows the height under the pointer: low in grooves, higher on peaks (Q-133). Timbre by the height's tag; heard from + Artistic on procedural and shader surfaces, in every state on a real mesh such as 433 Eros. Rule: rendering doc R8.9.1. | Deferred (D-39). The touch sources were deleted with the haptics; the tone stream (rendering doc R8.8) stays proposed for the Track path voice. |
| Track path sound | "Hear the Shift" in the Track tool: one tick per exposure (tagged like the exposure, pitch from λ, pan from the drawn x), a soft path voice in the Estimated timbre (Horizons path, `model_dependent`, silent in Measured only) and a quiet reference click with a visible mute (Q-134). Rule: rendering doc R8.9.2; the tool itself is A19 ([track.md](moving-objects/track.md)) and A20 ([track-ui.md](moving-objects/track-ui.md)). | Not built. |
| Imagined drive | Procedural noise, gravel and thuds shaped by gravity, roughness and surface type from the card. Always labelled "Imagined". | In the mock-up only, unheard. Not built in the app: deferred by issue #11 (lowest priority). When built it stays outside the haptic driver (rendering doc §8.9). |
| Sound engine and buses | Every engine event plays as sound (haptics cut, D-39), with the on/off switch and volume in the Sound popover. Buses `events`, `spectrum` and `ambience` into one limiter; the ambience ducks −9 dB under data sounds (R13.9). Audio carries continuous strength (gain), pitch, stereo pan and provenance by timbre: clean tone for `measured`, softer tone for Estimated, grainy noise for `artistic` (D-10, rendering doc §8.9). | Driver built (issue #9; now `frontend/src/sound/voices.ts`); buses, ducking and the Sound popover built (#111); not yet heard on the A15. |
| Flyover soundscape and weather | The imagined sound of the place under the fly camera at "+ Artistic": wind, surf, rain, rivers, lava, gas roar, thunder, from the world engine's physics; captions and "What you hear" (R13). | Specified (R13); built in #112 to #117. A gated extra. |
| Spectrum chord | The whole spectrum at once as a 12-bin chord, toggled by a button (R13.12, Q-443). | Built in #115 (Q-446): `frontend/src/sound/spectrumChord.ts`, in Hear it and in the sky tool's Spectrum panel (Sweep \| Chord), with the Data \| Smooth A/B. Empty bins are refused; mutually exclusive with both sweeps. Tested on a fake AudioContext; not yet heard on the A15 (R12 #63). |
| AI sound clips | Post-event backlog (B-03). | Not built for the event. |
| Voice control | Gemini Live. Post-event backlog (B-02). | Not built for the event. |
